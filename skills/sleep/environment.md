# Environment — Bedroom, Noise, Light, Partners

Environment fixes are prevention and low-hanging fruit: cheap, fast, zero adherence burden. They do not treat chronic insomnia (SKILL.md Traps) — run them alongside protocols, not instead.

## Temperature

- Cool room, warm periphery: 16-19 °C (60-67 °F) suits most sleepers; the sleep trigger is the core-temperature DROP, so cold hands and feet block it — socks in a cold room are performance equipment.
- Warm shower or bath 1-2 h before bed speeds onset via the rebound core drop afterward (Haghayegh meta-analysis); immediately before bed is too late for the rebound.
- Hot sleeper / cold partner: duvets are individual (see Partners); fix persons, not the thermostat war.
- Hot nights without AC: cross-ventilation, cotton over synthetics, damp-towel pulse points; expect fragmented sleep and drop the guilt — heat fragmentation is physics, not failure.

## Light

- Darkness target: cannot see your hand at arm's length. Streetlight through curtains and charging LEDs both register through closed eyelids; blackout curtains or a contoured eye mask.
- Dim the last 2-3 h: overheads off, lamps low and warm. This is the everyday half of the light rules in SKILL.md rule 7.
- Dawn light is a free wake anchor: curtains that leak morning light (or a dawn-simulation alarm in dark winters) recruit the clock to the wake time.
- Screens: engagement rules, not spectrum theater — no feeds in bed, devices charge outside arm's reach. The phone's content does more damage than its blue channel for average adults (SKILL.md Where Experts Disagree).

## Noise

- Steady beats silent-but-fragile: it is noise TRANSITIONS that wake people. A fan or noise machine masks door slams and traffic spikes; pick a steady broadband source and keep it on all night, not on a timer that clicks off.
- Foam earplugs correctly inserted (rolled, pulled ear, held) cut most partner and street noise; alarm anxiety with earplugs → vibrating wrist alarm.
- Intermittent identifiable noise (neighbor's bass, barking) resists masking and breeds monitoring-for-the-noise arousal — treat the source or the room (move the bed, seal gaps) before treating the sleeper.

## Air

Stuffy rooms degrade sleep quality; a closed small bedroom with two sleepers accumulates CO2 all night. Door ajar or window cracked; if neither is possible, air the room before bed.

## Bed, Bedding, Clock

- Mattress evidence is thin: medium-firm is the defensible default; replace when mornings start with stiffness that fades after an hour up — that pattern points at the surface, not the spine.
- Hide the clock: face it away, phone across the room. Time-checking at 3am is arithmetic panic on demand (`insomnia.md` 3am playbook).
- Bed reserved for sleep and sex — the stimulus-control rule lives or dies on the bedroom setup: no desk-bed, no TV-facing-bed if insomnia is active.

## Partners

- Snoring partner: earplugs + masking for you tonight; for THEM, the SKILL.md Red Flags row — snoring with pauses or gasps is their referral, and "my partner sent me" is how most apnea gets diagnosed.
- Separate duvets (the Scandinavian setup) ends cover theft and temperature wars with zero intimacy cost; separate rooms for sleep is a legitimate performance choice, not a marriage verdict — reframe it as protecting both sleepers.
- Mismatched schedules or chronotypes: the later one enters dark and silent (phone dimmed, clothes staged outside), the earlier one wakes to a vibrating alarm. Staggered bedtimes with a shared wind-down beat forced synchrony.
- Restless partner (kicking, thrashing): bigger mattress or separate beds solves you; regular rhythmic leg kicking during their sleep is worth flagging for their clinician.

## Kids and Pets

- Pets in the bed wake light sleepers measurably; the honest test is 2 weeks with the pet outside the room and the diary as judge — many sleepers pass and keep the pet, some are stunned by the difference.
- Co-sleeping with kids is a family-values call, not a coaching target; what IS coachable: returning night-visiting kids to their own bed the same way every time, because variable response trains persistent visiting.
- Baby in the room → `populations.md` (new parents).

## Hotel and Travel Kit

Recreate the signals, not the room: eye mask, earplugs, the same wind-down order as home. Thermostat to the cool end, hang-tag the door, curtains clipped shut (the clip is the most valuable item in the kit). First-night sleep in a new place runs shallow for most people (first-night effect) — schedule accordingly and skip the panic.
