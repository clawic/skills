# Learner States — Frustration, Anxiety, Motivation

Affect is data: it predicts whether the next exchange lands. Read it from message behavior and adjust the teaching — never diagnose aloud ("you seem frustrated"), which forces the learner to manage your perception on top of the material.

## Signals and Plays

| State | Observable signals | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Frustration | Shorter replies, self-blame ("I'm just dumb"), sudden "let's skip this" | Shrink the step to a near-guaranteed win, land it, then rebuild; attribute difficulty to the material ("this trips everyone at first"), never to the learner |
| Anxiety | Hedged answers ("probably wrong, but..."), stalling before easy checks, apologizing for mistakes | Frame probes as designed-to-miss — failed attempts before study improve learning (pretesting, Kornell); normalize errors before asking, not after |
| Overconfidence | Skipping ahead, "yeah, I know this", declining checks | One far-transfer question: a clean answer earns the skip (two instant corrects → jump a tier), a miss recalibrates without a lecture |
| Boredom vs overload | Both present as silence — run the differential in `stuck.md` before adjusting anything | One application question: instant-correct = bored, blank = overloaded |
| Fatigue | Rising error rate on material handled cleanly earlier the same session | Stop new content; run the closing retrieval set early. A short session ending in successful recall beats a long one ending in misses |
| Checked-out compliance | Instant "makes sense", no questions, no misses because no real attempts | Require generation (Rule 3); a learner who never misses is not being measured |

## Motivation Maintenance

- Make progress visible with evidence, not praise: contrast today's performance with the topic log's early entries ("three weeks ago this question was a blank") — the log in `memory-template.md` exists partly for this.
- Praise strategy and process, not the person — person-praise ("you're so smart") backfires at the next failure (Mueller and Dweck).
- When energy dips, offer a choice between two valid next steps; choice restores engagement without surrendering the curriculum.
- Reconnect material to the learner's stated goal every few sessions; an unanswered "why am I learning this" erodes attendance before it erodes performance.
- Interleaving and desirable difficulty make measured performance drop while learning improves (`retention.md`) — say so in advance, or the learner reads the drop as their failure.

## Hard Boundaries

Persistent distress, panic around evaluation, or disclosures beyond ordinary study stress are outside the teaching mandate: acknowledge plainly, suggest a human professional, and do not run therapeutic protocols. Struggling with the material is yours to fix; struggling beyond it is not.
