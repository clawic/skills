# Reading the Scale — Trends, Water, and Plateaus

The scale measures mass, not fat: water swings 1-2 kg daily (rule 6) while a real 500 kcal/day deficit moves true mass only ~0.45 kg/week (500×7 ÷ 7700). Every trend claim runs on 7-day averages.

## Weigh-In Protocol

Same scale, same floor spot, same time (morning, after bathroom, before food/drink, minimal clothing). Daily weigh-ins + weekly averaging beat weekly weigh-ins: one weekly reading IS the noise. Users who find daily weighing stressful weigh 3-4×/week — the average still works. Panic over daily numbers despite explanation is a Red Flags signal, not a data problem.

## Why the Scale Jumped Overnight (say this before they spiral)

| Cause | Typical size | Clears in |
|---|---|---|
| Salty restaurant meal | +0.5-1.5 kg | 1-2 days |
| Carb refeed after low-carb days | +1-2 kg | 2-3 days — glycogen binds ~3 g water/g (rule 6) |
| New or unusually hard training | +0.5-1 kg | 2-4 days — repair inflammation |
| Late luteal / menstruation | +0.5-2 kg | with the phase — compare cycle-aligned weeks month-over-month, not week-to-week |
| Alcohol evening | dip, then rebound | ~2 days — read nothing during (`restaurants.md`) |
| Travel day / long flight | +0.5-1.5 kg | a few days |
| Constipation / gut content | +0.5-1 kg | variable |
| Illness, injury, or a new medication | +0.5-2 kg | with recovery — log loosely, read nothing; med-driven shifts → `safety.md` |

Overnight fat gain math: +1 kg of fat requires a ~7700 kcal surplus in a day — nearly impossible by eating. Overnight jumps are water; say so with the numbers.

## The First Week of a Deficit

Drops 1-2 kg of glycogen and water. Report it as such at the start; counting it as fat sets up week-two disappointment when the "rate" collapses to the real one. Mirror image: the first week off a deficit regains it (`maintenance.md`).

## The Stall Protocol (in order, no skipping)

1. **Confirm it is a stall:** 2+ full weeks of flat 7-day averages. Under two weeks is noise (see table above) — do nothing yet.
2. **Audit logging for drift:** weekends, untracked oils, drinks, bites-while-cooking typically hide 200-500 kcal/day. The Lichtman under-reporting base rate (`calibration.md`) says check this before anything else.
3. **Recalibrate TDEE** per rule 5 (`calibration.md`) — mass loss and adaptation may have moved the target.
4. **Only then adjust intake** by 100-200 kcal, or add activity instead (often the better lever for adherence).

Cutting first is the classic error: it stacks a bigger deficit onto what was probably water masking, then adaptation punishes it.

## The "Whoosh"

Coaching lore: water masks fat loss for weeks, then releases in a sudden 1-2 kg drop. The pattern shows up in real trend lines but has no established mechanism — never promise one, never wait for one. The trend line is the tool; the whoosh is an anecdote that sometimes visits.

## Losing Too Fast

Sustained loss >1.5 kg/week for 2+ weeks is a Red Flags row (gallstone and muscle-loss risk), not a win: recommend raising intake toward the 0.5-1.0%/week band even if the user is pleased. Exception windows that fix themselves: week one of a deficit (glycogen, above) and post-vacation normalization (`restaurants.md`).

## Smart-Scale Body-Fat Readings

Bioimpedance (BIA) scales estimate body fat from electrical resistance, which tracks hydration — the same water swings that move weight 1-2 kg daily move the body-fat readout by several percentage points. A "body fat up 2%" morning after a salty dinner is water, not fat; deliver the same overnight-jump explanation. Usable protocol: same conditions as the weigh-in protocol above, compare monthly averages only, and treat the direction as signal and the absolute number as decoration. Scan-grade tools (DEXA and similar) are better but only comparable against the same machine and protocol; a quarterly cadence is plenty. For week-to-week recomposition tracking, tape and photos (below) beat every consumer body-fat number.

## Beyond the Scale

When weight is flat but the goal is recomposition — or the user is close to goal and the scale has lost signal — switch instruments: waist at navel monthly, progress photos every 4 weeks, gym numbers (`maintenance.md`). The scale is one sensor, not the verdict.
