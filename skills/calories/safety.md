# Safety — Escalation Scripts and Medical Contexts

The Red Flags table in SKILL.md is the trigger; this file is what to say and do once a row fires. The tone target everywhere: factual, warm, zero drama — escalation delivered as care, not alarm.

## Response Scripts (adapt wording, keep the moves)

- **Logs below floor (3+ days/week):** state the floor and its reason before any suggestion — "Your last few days are under 1200/1500 kcal. Below that it's hard to get enough protein and micronutrients even with perfect food choices, which is why I don't set targets there. I'd rather slow the target rate than run under the floor. A clinician can supervise lower if there's a reason to go faster." Then raise the target; do not wait for agreement to stop endorsing sub-floor numbers.
- **Disordered-eating signals (guilt language, gram-panic, compulsive logging):** stop the numbers first, explain second — "I'm going to pause calorie feedback, because the pattern in how we're tracking looks like it's adding stress rather than removing it. That's a known failure mode of tracking, not a personal one." Then share the helpline (identical numbers to SKILL.md: ANAD 1-888-375-7767 US / BEAT 0808 801 0677 UK) and offer to keep helping with non-numeric support (meals eaten yes/no, how they felt).
- **Binge-restrict cycle:** never propose compensation ("make up for it tomorrow") — compensation IS the cycle. Script: "Skipping meals after a big night is the mechanism that produces the next big night. The move that breaks the loop is a normal next meal at the normal time." No calorie totals for the binge day; route to a clinician for the pattern.
- **Declining a request (minor, underweight, pregnancy deficit):** decline the deficit, not the person — "I can't help set a deficit here, because [growth needs / fetal energy demands / your current weight] makes restriction a medical decision. I can keep logging neutrally if your clinician sets the numbers." Always leave one door open: neutral logging under clinician numbers.

## Eating Disorder History in Remission

A disclosed ED history changes the product even when no active signal fires: calorie tracking itself is a documented relapse vector. Default to the lowest-number mode the goal allows — weekly weigh-in only, hand portions instead of counts, band-based maintenance (`maintenance.md`) — and confirm their treatment team knows they are tracking. Escalate to full stop on the first active signal; the threshold is lower than for a no-history user.

## Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

- Deficits are off the table without clinician oversight (SKILL.md Red Flags). What neutral logging supports if the clinician approves: energy needs run ABOVE pre-pregnancy maintenance — roughly +340 kcal/day in the 2nd trimester and +452 in the 3rd (IOM), and about +330-400 during established breastfeeding.
- The useful reframe when a pregnant user asks about weight: gain targets are set by pre-pregnancy BMI and tracked by their OB — the skill's job is food logging fidelity, never rate management.

## Diabetes and Insulin

- Carb counting for insulin dosing is a medical dosing activity — log carbs with the same rigor as calories, but the ratio, corrections, and targets belong to their care team.
- A calorie deficit changes insulin requirements; an unadjusted dose plus reduced intake is a hypoglycemia setup. Any target change gets the line "run this by whoever manages your insulin before starting."
- Type 2 not on insulin: standard tracking applies, with the med-adjustment caveat below.

## GLP-1 Agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide, and successors)

The fastest-growing tracking cohort, with an inverted risk profile — the danger is under-eating, not over-eating:

- Appetite suppression makes the floors (rule 4) and the protein target (rule 7) the numbers that matter; calories self-limit, protein does not. In the STEP 1 DEXA substudy (semaglutide), roughly 40% of weight lost was lean mass — protein at 1.6-2.2 g/kg plus resistance training (`gym`) is the countermeasure.
- Practical pattern: small frequent protein-dense meals; a user who "isn't hungry" and logs 900 kcal is a floor violation even though it feels effortless.
- Rate and dosing decisions stay with the prescriber; the skill tracks against clinician-set numbers and flags sustained sub-floor intake or >1.5 kg/week loss to the user for their next appointment.

## Medications and Conditions That Move Weight

Thyroid disease, corticosteroids, antipsychotics, lithium, some antidepressants and beta-blockers shift appetite, water, or expenditure. Two rules: never attribute a stall or gain to willpower when one of these is in play — measured TDEE (`calibration.md`) absorbs the math without needing to explain it; and med changes are clinician territory — the skill's contribution is a clean log the user can bring to the appointment.

## Weight-Class and Aesthetic Sport Cuts

Requests to "make weight" via dehydration, water loading, or sauna protocols get declined: acute dehydration cuts are a documented cause of athlete deaths and sit outside food tracking entirely. Offer the legitimate version — a gradual fat-loss phase to a weight that leaves ≤2-3% to manage near the event — and route the acute protocol to a qualified combat-sport or team dietitian.

## Resuming After a Stop

When a clinician clears a previously-stopped user: neutral logging mode — clinician's numbers, no self-derived targets, no praise or criticism of totals, summaries at the cadence the user chooses. Re-run the Red Flags screen after any long gap; remission status and meds change.
