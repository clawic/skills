# Islands — Choosing and Planning

Two archipelagos, opposite logic: Balearics = Mediterranean summer islands (much closes in winter); Canaries = subtropical, year-round, winter is the point.

## Which Balearic

| Island | Pick it for | Skip it if |
|------|------------|-----------|
| Mallorca | Variety: Tramuntana mountains + coves + Palma; most flights and infrastructure | You believe the Magaluf myth — that's one town on a huge island |
| Menorca | Families, calm, the best coves, quiet | You want nightlife — there barely is any |
| Ibiza | Clubs June-Sep AND a quiet hippie north | Budget travel in August — everything is expensive |
| Formentera | Caribbean-clear water, slow pace | You need an airport — ferry only (30min from Ibiza) |

## Which Canary

| Island | Pick it for |
|------|------------|
| Tenerife | Teide volcano, biggest infrastructure; green north, resort south |
| Gran Canaria | Maspalomas dunes, Las Canteras urban beach, LGBTQ+ scene |
| Lanzarote | Volcanic landscapes, César Manrique architecture, La Geria wine |
| Fuerteventura | The best beaches, constant wind — kite/windsurf |
| La Palma | Hiking, stargazing (Roque de los Muchachos observatories) |
| La Gomera | Garajonay laurel forest, walking, silence |
| El Hierro | Remote, diving (La Restinga), off every radar |

## Getting There and Around

- **Flights** from the mainland are cheap and frequent (Vueling, Ryanair, Volotea) — the default for both archipelagos
- **Ferries to the Balearics** (Baleària, Trasmed) from Barcelona, Valencia, Dénia: 2-8h, and you can bring a car
- **Canaries by ferry from the mainland** takes 1.5-2 days — only if the crossing is the plan
- **Inter-island:** Balearics by ferry; Canaries by Binter/Canaryfly flights and Fred. Olsen/Armas ferries
- **Rent a car everywhere**, with three exceptions: a Palma-only city stay, Ibiza club trips (taxis), Formentera (scooter or bike is enough)

## Season Logic

| When | Balearics | Canaries |
|------|-----------|----------|
| June, September | Same sea as August, half the crowds — the smart window | Fine, quieter |
| July-August | Peak: book accommodation 2-3 months ahead; cove parking full by 9am | High season but less extreme |
| May, October | Quiet, sea still cool | Very good |
| Nov-April | Much closes (especially Menorca, Formentera) | The point: 20-25°C while the mainland is cold |

**Canaries caveat:** calima (Saharan dust haze) can arrive any time — flights occasionally delayed, views gone for a day or two.

## Traps

| Trap | Do instead |
|------|-----------|
| Boat-party or club tickets sold on the street | Official channels; street tickets oversell or don't exist |
| Driving to Es Trenc or Cala Macarella at noon in August | Arrive 9am or take the bus/boat options |
| All-inclusive resort keeping you inside | The food you came for is in the villages — get out at least for lunches |
| Booking Menorca or Formentera in November expecting summer services | Half of everything is shut; go for silence or change island |
