# Granada

## The Alhambra (The Ticket Problem)

The most visited monument in Spain sells out first. Solve it before anything else about the trip.

- **Book on the official site** (tickets.alhambra-patronato.es) 2-3 months ahead in season — book the day your dates are fixed, before flights if the Alhambra anchors the trip
- **General ticket:** €15-20. The Nasrid Palaces entry is a timed 30-minute slot printed on the ticket — miss the slot and you do not get in, no exceptions
- **Sold out?** In order of value: night visit (separate ticket, Nasrid Palaces lit, fewer people), Gardens+Generalife ticket (no Nasrid Palaces but still worth going), official guided tours (hold their own allotments), Granada Card
- **Time:** 3-4 hours; it's a fortified hill — real shoes, water in summer
- **Generalife gardens** peak in May; go early morning or last slot for light and space
- **Free views without any ticket:** Mirador de San Nicolás (sunset, packed, buskers) or Mirador de los Carvajales (fewer people)

## Free Tapas (The Real System)

Granada, Jaén, Almería, León: order a drink, a free tapa arrives. In Granada it works at almost every bar — three rounds is dinner for the price of three drinks (€9-12).

| Bar | Area | Known for |
|-----|------|-----------|
| Bodegas Castañeda | Centro | Classic, vermouth from the barrel |
| Los Diamantes | Navas (several branches) | Fried fish |
| La Bella y La Bestia | Centro/Elvira | Huge tapas, student crowd |
| Bar Ávila | Realejo | Local, generous |

**Rule:** the tapa comes with the round — you don't order it, you receive it. Moving bars every 1-2 rounds is the local pattern.

## Neighborhoods

| Neighborhood | What it is | Note |
|--------|-----------|------|
| **Albaicín** | Moorish quarter, UNESCO | Steep alleys, miradores; wear grippy shoes, skip with strollers |
| **Sacromonte** | Cave houses, zambra flamenco | Uphill from Albaicín; go for a show, not just a stroll |
| **Realejo** | Old Jewish quarter | Street art (El Niño de las Pinturas), tapas |
| **Centro** | Cathedral, Capilla Real | Isabella and Ferdinand's tombs in the Capilla Real |

**Calle Calderería Nueva:** the "little Morocco" street — teterías (tea houses), pastries. Pick one without menu photos.

## Flamenco

Granada's form is the **zambra** — danced in Sacromonte caves, older and rawer than tablao flamenco.

| Venue | Type | Note |
|-------|------|------|
| Cueva de la Rocío | Sacromonte cave | Classic zambra, book ahead |
| Zambra María la Canastera | Sacromonte cave | Historic family cave |
| Jardines de Zoraya | Albaicín | Restaurant-tablao, solid performers |
| Peña la Platería | Albaicín | One of Spain's oldest flamenco clubs; check performance nights |

## When to Go

| Season | Reality |
|-------|----------|
| Spring, fall | Best — Alhambra gardens in bloom, walkable heat |
| Summer | 35-40°C afternoons but drier than Seville; the city sits at ~700m, nights cool off |
| Winter | Cold, occasional snow on the hills; ski-plus-Alhambra combo works |

## Traps

| Trap | Do instead |
|------|-----------|
| "Skip-the-line Alhambra" from street resellers | Official site or official guided tour only |
| Mirador de San Nicolás at sunset with pockets open | It's a pickpocket spot; phone in front pocket |
| Rosemary sprig pressed into your hand near the cathedral | It's not free — decline hands-in-pockets and walk on |
| Eating on Plaza Nueva terraces | Two streets in, the free-tapa system resumes |
