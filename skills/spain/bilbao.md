# Bilbao

Post-industrial reinvention story: a shipyard city that became a design capital. Use it as the Basque base — cheaper beds than San Sebastián, better transport hub, and the pintxos are nearly as good at two-thirds the price.

## Guggenheim (Do It Right)

- **The building is the show** — Gehry's titanium exterior, Puppy (flower dog) and Maman (spider) are free from outside; budget the interior only if the current exhibitions interest you (check before buying)
- **Closed most Mondays** (open Mondays in peak summer) — verify on the official site before planning your day around it
- **Time:** 2-3 hours interior; go at opening to have the atrium empty
- **Best exterior photo:** across the river from the Deusto side, or from the Salve bridge at dusk

## Pintxos: Casco Viejo and Plaza Nueva

Same mechanics as San Sebastián (order, eat standing, pay at the end, move on — see `san-sebastian.md`). The epicenter is **Plaza Nueva**:

| Bar | Known for |
|-----|-----------|
| Gure Toki | Modern pintxos, consistently the plaza's best |
| Café Bar Bilbao | Classics, tiled interior |
| Sorginzulo | Gildas, crowd spills into the plaza |
| Víctor Montes | Historic, ham and txakoli |

- **Txikiteo** is the local ritual: short wines (txikitos) or small beers (zuritos), one per bar, moving in a fixed friend group — join the flow, don't camp a barstool
- **La Ribera market** (riverbank): one of Europe's largest covered markets; gastro bars at the end — go before 14:00 while stalls still work
- **Café Iruña** (Jardines de Albia): Moorish-tiled 1903 interior, famous lamb skewers (pinchos morunos) grilled at the back

## Beyond the Old Town

| Spot | Why |
|------|-----|
| Artxanda funicular | 3 minutes up, full city panorama — go late afternoon |
| Azkuna Zentroa (Alhóndiga) | Wine warehouse turned Starck-designed cultural center; rooftop pool with glass floor |
| Zubizuri bridge + Isozaki towers | The white Calatrava footbridge, slippery when wet (locals joke about it for a reason) |
| Bilbao La Vieja | Former rough quarter, now street art and alternative bars — evening, not late night alone |

## Day Trips

| Destination | How | What |
|---------|------|-----|
| San Juan de Gaztelugatxe | Car/Bizkaibus ~40min + walk | 241-step hermitage islet ("Dragonstone"); free timed permit required in season — book online days ahead |
| Getxo + Portugalete | Metro | Vizcaya Hanging Bridge (UNESCO), gondola crossing, mansions promenade |
| Mundaka | Train (Euskotren) ~45min | World-famous surf left; Urdaibai estuary views |
| Gernika | Train ~40min | Peace Museum, the Assembly House and oak; Monday market is the lively day |
| San Sebastián | Bus ~1.5h | Cheaper to sleep in Bilbao, pintxos-crawl there |

## Getting Around

- **Metro** (Norman Foster's glass "fosterito" entrances) covers the city and both coasts to the beaches — Barik card cuts every fare
- **No beach in the city**: metro to Sopelana or Plentzia (~30min) for sand and surf
- **Airport:** Bizkaibus A3247 to Moyúa, ~25min, runs early to late — skip the taxi unless it's 4am

## When to Go

| Period | Reality |
|--------|----------|
| May-Oct | Best odds with the weather — but it rains year-round; jacket always |
| Aste Nagusia (late Aug, 9 days) | Semana Grande: konpartsak street parties, fireworks nightly — book beds well ahead or avoid the dates |
| Winter | Fine for a museum-and-pintxos weekend; short days, frequent rain |

## Traps

| Trap | Do instead |
|------|-----------|
| Planning Monday around the Guggenheim | Check opening first; Mondays often closed outside summer |
| Expecting a beach walkable from downtown | Metro to Sopelana/Plentzia — the city riverfront is for strolling, not swimming |
| Judging Bilbao by the station surroundings | The good city is Plaza Nueva, Abando, the riverfront — give it an evening |
| Driving to Gaztelugatxe at noon in summer | Permit slots and parking vanish; go first slot of the morning |
