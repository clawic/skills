# Málaga

Andalusia's airport gateway — and no longer just a transfer point. The center earns 1-2 days: serious museums, the best market bar scene in the south, and beach-shack sardines you can't get inland.

## Museums

| Museum | Note |
|--------|------|
| Museo Picasso | The main event — he was born here; book online in season |
| Casa Natal (birthplace) | Separate, smaller; only for completists |
| Carmen Thyssen | Andalusian 19th-century painting, quiet |
| Centre Pompidou (the Cube) | Small branch at the port; the colored cube itself is the photo |
| CAC (contemporary) | Free entry |

**Alcazaba + Gibralfaro:** combined ticket is the play; the Roman theatre below the Alcazaba is free. Climb (or bus 35) to Gibralfaro castle for the port panorama — go late afternoon, not midday heat.

## Espetos (The Reason to Come Hungry)

Sardine skewers grilled over olive-wood fires in beached boats — a Málaga monopoly.

- **Where:** chiringuitos in **Pedregalejo** and **El Palo** (bus/30-min seafront walk east), not the city-center imitations
- **When:** sardines peak in the warm months — locals follow the "months without an R" rule (May-August); off-season the espeto is frozen stock
- **El Tintero** (El Palo): auction-style — waiters circle shouting dishes, you flag what you want, plates are tallied at the end; chaotic and worth it
- Order espetos + ensalada malagueña + a cold Victoria beer; pay per skewer (a few euros each)

## Eating in the Center

- **Atarazanas market:** the stained-glass hall; tapas bar at the back — arrive before 14:00, stalls close after lunch
- **El Pimpi:** famous bodega, touristy but the sweet Málaga wine from barrels is real; go for one glass, eat elsewhere
- **Calle Larios** is for strolling, not eating — walk two streets off it for locals' bars
- **Soho district:** street art (MAUS project) + newer restaurants between center and port

## Beaches

| Beach | Note |
|-------|------|
| La Malagueta | Urban, convenient, unremarkable — fine for a swim, not the day's plan |
| Pedregalejo | Smaller coves + the espeto chiringuitos: beach day and lunch solved together |
| Nerja (Burriana) | 1h bus east — the best beach day in the province (→ beaches.md) |

## Day Trips

| Destination | How | What |
|---------|------|-----|
| Caminito del Rey | Train/car ~40min | Cliff walkway 100m up; one-way route, book ~2 months ahead (→ experiences.md) |
| Ronda | Train ~2h / bus 1h45 | Puente Nuevo gorge, old bullring; leave before the midday tour buses arrive |
| Nerja + Frigiliana | Bus ~1h | Caves + the prettiest white village within easy reach |
| Antequera | Train ~30min | El Torcal karst landscape, Bronze Age dolmens (UNESCO) |
| Marbella old town | Bus ~1h | Orange-tree plaza charm — the old town, not the marina |

## When to Go

| Period | Reality |
|--------|----------|
| Oct-May | The point of Málaga: mild when the rest of Europe isn't |
| Semana Santa | Huge thrones (tronos) carried by hundreds — more festive, less solemn than Sevilla's |
| Feria de Málaga (mid-Aug) | Day fair takes over the center with sherry and dancing — join it or avoid the week entirely |
| July-Aug | Hot, packed, coast prices double |

## Traps

| Trap | Do instead |
|------|-----------|
| Staying in Torremolinos "to see Málaga" | Different planet; sleep in Centro/Soho and day-trip the coast |
| Espetos in a city-center restaurant in January | Wrong place, wrong season — Pedregalejo, warm months |
| Renting a car for the city | Center is pedestrianized, garages expensive; car pays off only for white villages (→ transport.md) |
| "Free" rooftop views pitch near the cathedral | The Alcazaba/Gibralfaro ticket is a few euros and is the actual view |
