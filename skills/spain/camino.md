# Camino de Santiago

Route choice, Compostela rules, albergue mechanics, and the mistakes that end pilgrimages in week one.

## Which Route

| Route | Start (full) | Character | Pick it if |
|-------|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| Francés | St-Jean-Pied-de-Port (~780km, ~33 days) | Most infrastructure, most social, most crowded | First Camino, walking alone but wanting company |
| Portugués | Porto (~240km, ~12 days) | Flatter, coastal variant available, 2nd most popular | Less time, gentler terrain |
| Norte | Irún (~825km) | Coastal cliffs, more climbing, fewer albergues | Fit, want scenery over sociability |
| Primitivo | Oviedo (~320km) | The original route; hardest per km, quietest; joins the Francés at Melide | Experienced walkers wanting mountains and silence |
| Inglés | Ferrol (~115km, 5-6 days) | Short but qualifies for the Compostela | One free week, want the certificate legitimately |
| Vía de la Plata | Sevilla (~1,000km) | Long, hot, sparse | Veterans; never in summer |

## Compostela Rules (The Certificate)

- **Minimum:** last **100km on foot** (or 200km by bike) into Santiago — that's Sarria on the Francés, Tui on the Portugués, Ferrol on the Inglés
- **Credential (pilgrim passport):** get it from a Camino association, cathedral office, or major albergues at the start; stamped along the way — **two stamps per day within the final 100km**
- Collect stamps at albergues, churches, bars — any of them count
- In Santiago: the Pilgrim's Office issues the Compostela; in season, register in the online queue system early in the day

## Albergues (How Sleeping Works)

| Type | Price | Rules |
|------|-------|-------|
| Municipal/parish | ~€8-12 | No reservations, first-come, one night only, pilgrims with credential |
| Private albergue | ~€12-18 | Bookable — the pressure valve in high season |
| Pensión/hotel | €30+ | For the nights you need a door that closes |

- **High-season reality (May-Sep, Francés final 100km):** municipal beds can run out by early-to-mid afternoon. Either start walking by 6:30-7:00 to arrive by 13:00-14:00, or book private albergues a day or two ahead
- **Pilgrim menu:** ~€10-15 for three courses with wine, everywhere on the Francés
- Lights out ~22:00, everyone leaves by ~8:00 — earplugs are not optional equipment

## Sarria → Santiago in 5 Days (The Classic Minimum)

| Day | Stage | ~km |
|-----|-------|-----|
| 1 | Sarria → Portomarín | 22 |
| 2 | Portomarín → Palas de Rei | 25 |
| 3 | Palas de Rei → Arzúa | 29 (splittable at Melide — eat pulpo there) |
| 4 | Arzúa → O Pedrouzo | 19 |
| 5 | O Pedrouzo → Santiago | 20 |

Day-distance rule: 20-25km/day is sustainable for a fit non-hiker; plan the first two days shorter, not longer.

## Gear and Body

- **Pack weight:** aim under ~10% of body weight — the classic pilgrim guideline; every extra kilo shows up in your knees by day 4
- **Shoes:** broken-in trail shoes beat new boots; the #1 first-week dropout cause is tendinitis and blisters from overreaching in new footwear
- **Blister kit** (needle, thread, Compeed), poncho for Galicia (it will rain), sleeping bag liner for albergues
- **Luggage transfer exists:** Correos "Paq Mochila" moves your bag stage-to-stage (~€5-7/stage) — using it is not cheating, it's how many finish

## When to Walk

| Period | Reality |
|--------|----------|
| May-June, September | The answer: walkable weather, open albergues, manageable crowds |
| July-August | Hot on the meseta, bed race on the Francés |
| October | Good, quieter; Galicia rain begins |
| Nov-April | Francés stays open (reduced); Norte/Primitivo albergues largely close |

## In Santiago

- **Pilgrim mass** daily at the cathedral; the **botafumeiro** (giant censer) is NOT guaranteed — it swings on feast days or when a group has paid for it; ask at the Pilgrim's Office which masses that week
- Hug-the-Apostle and crypt visits queue separately from mass
- Celebration meal: pulpo á feira and albariño (→ food-guide.md); many pilgrims add the bus to Fisterra (the "end of the world") for the true full stop

## Traps

| Trap | Do instead |
|------|-----------|
| 30km+ days in the first week | Cap at 20-25km until your body votes; the injury curve is front-loaded |
| New boots bought the week before | Walk 50+ km in your shoes at home first |
| Expecting solitude from Sarria | It's the busiest corridor in Europe; start stages at dawn for quiet, or choose the Primitivo |
| Card-only wallet in small villages | Many bars and municipal albergues are cash-only; carry small bills |
| Skipping the credential until Galicia | Without consistent stamps in the last 100km, no Compostela |
