# Valencia

## Paella Rules (This Is the Home Turf)

- **Lunch only.** A kitchen serving dinner paella is cooking for tourists — the national rule is strictest here
- **Paella valenciana** = chicken, rabbit, garrofó beans. Seafood versions are arroz a banda or arroz negro. "Paella mixta" (meat + seafood) is a tourist dish no Valencian orders
- **Fresh takes 20-40 minutes** and is ordered for 2+ people. If it arrives in 10 minutes, it was reheated
- **Sunday lunch paella is the family ritual** — book Sundays, or you're eating at the bar

## Fallas (15-19 March)

- Giant satirical monuments built all year, burned the night of the 19th (La Cremà)
- **Mascletà:** daily 14:00 at Plaza del Ayuntamiento through March — a coordinated gunpowder concert; arrive by 13:15 or listen from three streets back
- Firecrackers go off 24/7; sleeping in the center is not really possible — book months ahead, or stay outside the center on metro lines
- Not a stadium event: wander the neighborhoods, every one has its own falla and street party

| El Carmen | Old town, street art, nightlife |
| Ruzafa | Hipster, brunch, terraces |
| Cabanyal | Old fishermen's quarter, reborn, near the beach |
| Ciutat de les Arts | The Calatrava complex |

- **Turia gardens:** a 9km park in a dried riverbed crossing the whole city — rent a bike (flattest big city in Spain, best cycled)
- **Central Market:** maybe the best food market in Spain — go before 12:00 while it's a working market
- **City of Arts and Sciences:** walking the complex is free and the architecture is the show; pay only for Oceanogràfic (largest aquarium in Europe) or the Science Museum with kids

## Horchata

Only authentic here (tiger-nut milk, D.O. from Alboraia). Order **fartons** for dunking. Summer drink, served ice-cold.

| Horchatería | Where |
|-------------|-------|
| Horchatería Daniel | Alboraia (the capital of horchata) |
| Horchatería Santa Catalina | Centro — 200 years making it the same way |

## When

| Period | Reality |
|--------|----------|
| March | Fallas — incredible and exhausting; book far ahead or skip the dates deliberately |
| April-June, Sep-Oct | Best weather-to-crowds ratio |
| July-August | Humid 30-35°C; beach crowds, city fine by morning/evening |
| Late Oct-Nov | Storm season (DANA downpours can be severe) — keep an indoor plan B |
