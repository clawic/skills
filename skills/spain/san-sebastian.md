# San Sebastián / Donostia

## The Basics

- **Language:** Spanish + Basque (bilingual signs)
- **Climate:** It rains a lot. Bring a rain jacket.
- **Expensive:** One of the priciest cities in Spain
- **Gastronomy:** Possibly the best in Europe

## Pintxos (Not Tapas)

### How It Works
1. Go to the bar
2. Order pintxo(s) by pointing
3. Eat standing
4. Pay at the end (they keep the toothpicks or just trust you)
5. Move to another bar (1-2 pintxos per place)

### Recommended Crawl
**Thursday or Friday night**, 20:00-23:00:
1. La Cuchara (hot)
2. Gandarias (meat)
3. Ganbara (mushrooms)

5. Bar Nestor (tortilla if you make it)

## Cider Houses (Seasonal)

**When:** January-April
**Where:** Outskirts (Astigarraga)
**What it is:** Dinner at a cider house with a fixed menu

**Typical menu (~€35-40):**
- Cod tortilla
- Cod with peppers
- Txuleta (rib steak)
- Cheese with quince
- Walnuts
- Cider at will (you pour it yourself from the barrel)

**Well-known cider houses:**
- Petritegi
- Zelaia
- Alorrenea

**Booking:** Mandatory, weeks ahead in season.

## Tips

1. **Booking pintxos is impossible** — you go and wait
2. **Thursday night** — locals do the crawl
3. **Payment:** Many bars are cash only
4. **Basque:** "Kaixo" (hi), "Eskerrik asko" (thanks)
5. **Umbrella always** — it rains without warning
