# Comeback — Layoffs, Illness, Injury Return

Re-entry prescriptions after every kind of interruption. The layoff discount math (skip / re-entry / −10% per week, floor 50%) is canonical in SKILL.md Rule 6; detraining rates and the muscle-memory effect in Recovery and Readiness.

## Layoff Table (Rule 6 applied)

| Gap | Lifting prescription | Cardio prescription |
|---|---|---|
| < 1 week | Skip missed sessions, resume as planned; never stack two into one | Resume as planned |
| 1-2 weeks | Last logged loads, no jump; normal progression resumes next session | Last weekly minutes, no hard sessions the first week |
| 2-4 weeks | −10% load per week missed; rebuild ~10% per week | Resume at the same discount on weekly minutes; Zone 2 only for week one |
| 1-3 months | Floor case: 50% of last logged loads, full-body 3x/week regardless of prior split; rebuild ~10% per week | Restart at ~50% of prior minutes, then +10% weekly |
| 3+ months | Treat as a new trainee (SKILL.md Quick Reference: no training history), beginner linear progression | Rebuild the base before any intervals: 4+ weeks Zone 2 first |

Worked example (canonical in Rule 6): 3 weeks off a 100 kg lift → resume near 70 kg, add back ~10% per week. The muscle-memory effect means the rebuild takes weeks, not the months the strength originally took — the discount is cheap insurance, and connective tissue re-adapts slower than muscle, which is exactly what the ramp protects.

## Illness

- Neck rule: symptoms above the neck only (runny nose, mild sore throat) → training is optional at reduced intensity, Zone 2 or technique work, no hard sets. Symptoms below the neck (fever, chest congestion, body aches, GI) → full rest, no exceptions.
- Fever: no training until fever-free for 24h without medication. Post-viral fatigue is real: first week back at half volume even when the gap was short, then the Rule 6 row for the total gap length.
- Chest symptoms or palpitations persisting after recovery → Red Flags table, clinician before intensity.

## Injury Return (pain-monitoring model)

For niggles and cleared rehab returns — anything in the Red Flags table goes to a clinician first, and a clinician's protocol outranks this section.

- Pain rule (Silbernagel model): pain up to 5/10 during and after loading is acceptable **if** it settles to baseline by the next morning. Above 5/10, or worse the next morning → that load was too much; step back one rung.
- Loading ladder for the affected pattern, one rung per pain-free step, 2-4 sessions per rung:
  1. Isometrics in a pain-tolerable position (e.g. wall sit for knee pain)
  2. Slow-tempo, partial range, light load
  3. Full range, light load (around 50% of pre-injury working weight)
  4. Normal tempo, rebuild load on the Rule 6 ramp (~10% per week) while the pain rule holds
- Everything unaffected trains normally the whole time — "resting" the whole body for one cranky elbow is the classic dropout path. Substitute within the movement pattern to keep training around the injury.
- Plateau in rehab (pain not improving rung to rung across 2 weeks) → clinician, per the Red Flags structural-injury row.

## What Detrains at What Speed

Canonical rates in SKILL.md (strength holds ~3 weeks; VO2max drops within 2-4 weeks). Programming consequences:

- Strength survives short gaps; the endurance base is the perishable asset — after any 2+ week gap, weight cardio re-entry more conservatively than lifting.
- Work capacity (tolerating volume) fades faster than top-end strength: comeback weeks fail by total sets, not by load. First week back, cut set counts ~30% below the discounted plan even when loads feel fine.
- Movement skill barely detrains; soreness response un-adapts fast. The first novel-feeling session back will produce outsized DOMS (24-72h, canonical) — warn, and keep session one deliberately submaximal.

## Restart Psychology

- The log restarts as data, not as a verdict (SKILL.md Traps: no guilt-framing). Open with the discounted prescription, not with a review of the gap.
- Momentum rule: the first two weeks back optimize for sessions completed, not for progress. Attendance rebuilt first, loads second — the same attendance-first priority order SKILL.md Traps applies to splits.
