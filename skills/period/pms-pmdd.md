# PMS and PMDD — Tracking the Luteal Pattern

The agent's leverage here is unusual: prospective daily logging IS the clinical assessment tool. Memory overreports cyclicity; a two-cycle daily log settles what recall cannot.

## The Definitions That Gate Everything

- **PMS**: physical and mood symptoms confined to the luteal phase (ovulation to menses, ~10-16 days — SKILL.md rule 4), easing within a few days after bleeding starts. Common and, when mild, not a disorder.
- **PMDD**: the severe, function-impairing form. DSM-5 requires 5+ symptoms with at least one core mood symptom (mood lability, irritability, depressed mood, or anxiety), restricted to the luteal phase, impairing work or relationships, and — the part most people skip — confirmed by prospective daily ratings across 2 symptomatic cycles. It is far rarer than PMS: a few percent of menstruating women, not everyone with premenstrual irritability.
- **PME (premenstrual exacerbation)**: an underlying condition (depression, anxiety, ADHD) that worsens premenstrually but never fully remits. Different treatment path — and only a daily log distinguishes it from PMDD.

## The Distinguishing Test

A symptom-free (or near-free) follicular week is required for PMS/PMDD. Symptoms present all month that spike premenstrually = PME pattern. Either result routes to a clinician, but they are different conversations — the log determines which one she has.

## Running a Two-Cycle Assessment

Offer this only when she asks whether her symptoms are PMS/PMDD, or a clinician asked her to track:

1. Daily entry, ~1 minute, every day including good days (the good days are the diagnostic signal).
2. Rate each of her candidate symptoms 1-6 (DRSP-style: 1 = not at all, 6 = extreme) plus one interference line: did symptoms affect work, relationships, or daily life today?
3. Run 2 full cycles before reading anything. Mid-assessment interpretation defeats the design.
4. Read-out: chart severity against cycle day. Luteal-restricted with follicular remission → PMS/PMDD-consistent; continuous with premenstrual worsening → PME-consistent. Hand her the summary for the appointment (`privacy.md` sharing rules); never issue the diagnosis.

## What Helps (advise-level)

- Solid evidence, self-serve: regular aerobic exercise, stable sleep timing across the whole cycle (luteal sleep disruption amplifies mood symptoms).
- Effective but prescription-level: SSRIs (including luteal-phase-only dosing — a distinctive PMDD finding: they work within days, unlike in depression) and some combined contraceptives. Her log is what makes that appointment productive; the prescription is not this skill's call.
- Weak-evidence tier: calcium and vitamin B6 have small supportive trials; present as "some evidence," never as treatment.

## Hard Line

Premenstrual thoughts of self-harm are a recognized severe-PMDD presentation and a crisis, not a data point: crisis resources immediately, tracking suspended (SKILL.md Red Flags). Resume logging only if she chooses to after support is in place.

## Traps Specific to This File

- Calling any premenstrual annoyance PMDD — the impairment axis and the two-cycle confirmation are the definition, not the vibe.
- Starting the daily log during the symptomatic week and stopping when she feels better — the remission window is the evidence.
- Telling her mid-cycle "this is probably your luteal phase talking" — attribution stays hers (SKILL.md Traps), even during an assessment she opted into.
