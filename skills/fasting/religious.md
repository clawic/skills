# Religious Fasting — Observance Mode

Observance, not optimization. Never suggest extending, "improving", or converting a religious fast into a metabolic one; never editorialize on rulings. Exemptions and makeup obligations (qada, fidya) belong to the user's tradition and religious authority — out of scope, respected without commentary.

## Mode Switches (active when `observance` is set)

- Dawn/sunset boundaries beat any hour target; sunset closes the fast regardless of the clock.
- Dry fast: hydration prompts suspended entirely — prompting water during a dry fast is the observance-mode failure.
- Streaks count observance days kept; no protocol coaching, no progression suggestions.
- Exemptions (illness, travel, menstruation, pregnancy, age, breastfeeding) are respected and never second-guessed in either direction — not "you could still fast" and not "you should use the exemption" unless a Red Flags signal fires, where the exemption is exactly the point.

## Ramadan (dawn to sunset, dry, ~29-30 days)

| Concern | Play |
|---|---|
| Suhoor (pre-dawn meal) | Protein + slow carbs + fluids + salt with it. No mid-fast correction is possible, so sodium and water front-load here or nowhere |
| Iftar (sunset break) | Fluids and dates first, pause, then the meal — the traditional sequence independently matches refeed logic (`refeeding.md`): fluid + fast sugar + wait |
| Caffeine | Taper in the week BEFORE day 1 — mid-fast coffee is impossible and the day 1-3 withdrawal headache stacks on the fasting headache (`symptoms.md`) |
| Sleep | Compressed by suhoor and night prayers; expect afternoon dips — attribute fatigue to sleep before blaming the fast |
| Training | Post-iftar slot; fasted daytime training in a dry fast is the highest-risk combination in this skill (`training.md`) |
| Summer / hot climates | Long hours with zero water: symptoms escalate faster, thresholds for breaking are lower, and the illness exemption exists for exactly this |
| Medications on fixed schedules | Prescriber consult before the month starts, not during (`safety.md`) — dose timing around dawn/sunset is a clinical call |

## Other Observances

| Observance | Shape | Tracking note |
|---|---|---|
| Yom Kippur | ~25h, dry | Pre-fast meal follows the suhoor logic; caffeine taper ahead |
| Baha'i fast | 19 days, dawn to sunset, dry | Ramadan mode applies day by day |
| LDS Fast Sunday | ~24h, monthly | Water rules per the user's own practice — ask once, store the ruling |
| Ekadashi | Twice monthly; grain abstention in most traditions | Category abstention, not a clock — log what is abstained |
| Lent / Orthodox fasting | Weeks of food-category restriction | Not a clock fast at all: track the categories kept, never elapsed hours |
| Anything else | As the user defines it | Log their definition; never map it onto 16:8 vocabulary |

## Dry-Fast Safety Deltas

- No mid-fast correction exists: everything — fluid, sodium, medication timing — happens at the boundary meals. The rule 4 electrolyte protocol compresses into suhoor/iftar.
- Break-now signals, lower bar than water fasts: dizziness on standing, very dark urine, confusion, vomiting. The illness exemption is the tradition's own answer; invoking it is observance, not failure.
- Never log a medically-broken observance day as a failed streak — record it with the `observance` flag and move on.
