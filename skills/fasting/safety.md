# Safety — Conditions, Medications, Escalation

Depth behind the Red Flags table in SKILL.md. This file decides who the tracker must not coach and what suspends the protocols. The thresholds here are content: run them, never soften them. Never advise changing, skipping, or delaying any medication dose — that call belongs to the prescriber, always.

## Do Not Track Fasts

| Who | Why | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Pregnant or breastfeeding | Nutritional demand is continuous; fasting is contraindicated | Red Flags row: no tracking, refer to their provider |
| Eating disorder, current or history | Fasting structure is a relapse vehicle | Stop tracking; offer support (text "NEDA" to 741741, Crisis Text Line); no numbers back |
| Type 1 diabetes | Hypoglycemia and DKA risk without insulin titration | Clinician-designed plan only; decline solo coaching |
| BMI <18.5 or recent unintended weight loss | No reserves; NICE refeeding high-risk criterion (`refeeding.md`) | Clinician; do not log fasts |
| Under 18 | Growth phase; no pediatric fasting evidence base | Decline; suggest regular meal structure instead |
| Active gallstone disease | Fasting concentrates bile and provokes attacks | Clinician first |

## Clinician Before Fasting (track only alongside a confirmed plan)

Type 2 diabetes on insulin or sulfonylureas · heart, kidney, or liver disease · age 65+ · gallstone history · gout · prior refeeding syndrome · three or more daily prescription medications. For these, the agent's role shrinks to logging what the clinician approved — no protocol design, no progression suggestions.

## Medication Table

| Medication | Interaction with fasting | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Insulin, sulfonylureas (glipizide, glyburide) | Dose calibrated to eating; fasting stacks hypo risk | Rule 6 verbatim: glucose <70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L) → break, 15 g fast carb, recheck in 15 min. Doses are the prescriber's domain |
| Metformin | Low hypo risk alone; GI upset on an empty stomach | "Take with food" label → timing question for the pharmacist, not the tracker |
| SGLT2 inhibitors (-gliflozin) | Euglycemic DKA risk rises with fasting/ketosis | Nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain is urgent EVEN with normal glucose — clinician now |
| GLP-1 agonists (semaglutide etc.) | Appetite already suppressed; fasting compounds under-eating | Watch protein actually eaten in the window (`training.md` target); low solo hypo risk |
| Diuretics, BP medications | Compound orthostatic dizziness; the rule 4 sodium protocol conflicts with the diuretic's purpose | Clinician approves the electrolyte protocol before any extended fast |
| Warfarin | Vitamin K intake swings shift INR | Keep green-vegetable intake steady across window and non-window days |
| Lithium | Dehydration concentrates serum lithium toward toxicity | Extra caution on any fast; dry fasts (`religious.md`) need prescriber sign-off |
| Levothyroxine | Empty stomach is the label instruction | Fasting-compatible; take with water inside the fast, keeps the clock |
| NSAIDs | Gastric irritation without a food buffer | Prefer dosing inside the eating window |
| Any "take with food" label | Skipping the dose to protect a fast trades a logged streak for a therapeutic failure | Never advise skip/delay (`tracking.md`); prescribed meds keep the clock |

## Condition Notes

- **Gallbladder**: fasting plus rapid weight loss raises gallstone risk; recurring right-upper-abdomen pain, especially after break meals → clinician (`refeeding.md`, `symptoms.md`).
- **Gout**: ketones compete with urate excretion, so uric acid rises during fasts — flare risk on extended fasts; a gout history moves 24h+ fasts to clinician-first.
- **Kidney disease**: the rule 4 potassium target is dangerous with impaired excretion — the electrolyte protocol itself needs sign-off, not just the fast.
- **Diabetes, any type**: glucose checks before and during fasts; the morning rise from dawn phenomenon is cortisol, not a broken fast (`metrics.md`).

## Disordered-Eating Screen (patterns, not diagnosis)

Any of these shifts the interaction from tracking to support:

- Compensatory fasts scheduled after "bad" eating (`refeeding.md` names this loop)
- Fasting framed as punishment, or fear of eating inside the window
- Hiding food or fasts from household members
- Falling weight trajectory plus resistance to stepping down (`weight-loss.md` rate review)

Action: stop logging, return no streaks or numbers, offer support (text "NEDA" to 741741, Crisis Text Line), keep the tone neutral — no lecture, no diagnosis.

## Escalation Ladder

| Signal | Level | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Single mild symptom | Self-serve | Symptom chain in `symptoms.md` |
| Two+ of dizziness, palpitations, confusion, vomiting | Break now | Stop triaging; break the fast; clinician if not clearly resolving |
| Any Red Flags row | Suspend protocols | Run the row's Action; route to a clinician |
| Chest pain, fainting with injury, confusion or slurred speech | Emergency | Emergency services, immediately — not a message thread |
