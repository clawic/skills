# Training — Exercising Around and During Fasts

Fasted training is a scheduling problem, not a virtue. The goal: sessions the user keeps, muscle preserved, and a hard stop for the symptoms that end a workout.

## Session Placement

| Protocol | Best slot | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 16:8 / 18:6 | Last 1-2h of the fast, window opens right after | Train, then the first meal is the recovery meal — no timing gymnastics |
| OMAD | 1-2h before the meal | The single meal doubles as post-workout; morning OMAD training means a long unfed recovery — accept lighter loads |
| 5:2 / ADF | Hard sessions on fed days | Fast days: walks and mobility; the 500-600 kcal budget cannot fuel intensity |
| Extended >=24h | Walking and mobility only | Strength and HIIT stop at 24h (`extended.md`) — no exceptions for feeling good |
| Morning trainer, evening window | Keep intensity moderate, sodium before the session | Or shift `eating_window` earlier — adherence decides, not dogma |

## What Holds and What Degrades Fasted

- **Zone 2 / easy cardio**: fine fasted at any common protocol length; fat oxidation covers it.
- **Strength, low-moderate volume**: holds for most after a 2-3 week adaptation; early sessions feel flat — that is adaptation, not damage (`protocols.md` hold-2-weeks rule).
- **HIIT and glycolytic work**: first to suffer — glycogen-dependent by nature. Schedule fed, or accept measured intent: intervals, not maximal.
- **Max attempts and technical skill work**: not fasted. PRs and high-skill practice want fuel and full alertness.

## Stop the Session Now

Dizziness or tunnel vision · form breakdown on loaded lifts · cold sweat with shakiness (hypoglycemia pattern → `symptoms.md`; a medicated diabetic here is rule 6) · palpitations (Red Flags row — break the fast too, not just the workout).

## Supplements vs the Clock

Canonical rulings live in the gray-zone table (`tracking.md`); training-relevant rows:

| Supplement | Fast status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Plain creatine (0 kcal) | Keeps | Timing-agnostic — daily dose whenever, fast or window |
| Caffeine (black coffee, plain pre-fast tabs) | Keeps | Watch empty-stomach nausea (`symptoms.md`) |
| Unsweetened electrolytes | Keeps | Sodium before a fasted session blunts the dizziness pattern |
| BCAA / EAA | Breaks | Insulin-triggering by design — the marketing says "fasted-safe", the physiology says fed |
| Sweetened or caloric pre-workout | Breaks | Sweet taste breaks strict; calories break both definitions |
| Protein shake | Breaks | It IS the break — take it after training as the window opener, by design |

## Muscle Preservation

- Daily protein 1.6-2.2 g/kg (ISSN range), eaten inside the window. This is the canonical protein target for the whole skill. OMAD rarely carries the top of that range in one sitting — count it, don't assume it.
- Resistance training 2+ sessions/week is the anti-catabolic lever. Shortening fasts to "save muscle" while not lifting solves the wrong variable.
- Training quality collapsing across weeks — loads down, sessions skipped, recovery gone — is a step-down trigger (`protocols.md`), not something to push through. One flat week is noise; a month-long slide is data.
