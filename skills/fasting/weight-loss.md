# Weight Loss — Rate, Water Weight, Plateaus

## What Fasting Actually Does

The window compresses eating time and usually cuts intake; the deficit does the losing. Fasting is an adherence tool with metabolic side benefits, not a metabolism hack — set that expectation at the first weight question, because every plateau conversation below depends on it.

## Rate

- Sustainable after week 1: 0.25-1% of body weight per week. Worked: 80 kg → 0.2-0.8 kg/week; 10 kg to lose ≈ 3-12 months, not 6 weeks.
- Week 1 is glycogen and water, not fat: 1 g glycogen binds ~3 g water, and ~300-500 g of stored glycogen means a 1-2 kg swing that returns at any refeed. Neither the first-week whoosh nor the post-fast bounce is fat — say it before the scale does.
- Sustained loss >1.5%/week beyond the first weeks triggers the falling-trajectory review; paired with any disordered-eating pattern it is a Red Flags row (`safety.md`).

## Weigh-In Protocol

Same scale, morning, post-void, before food or coffee. Judge only the 7-day rolling average: single readings swing ±1-2% on fluid, glycogen, and gut content alone. During an extended fast the scale lies wholesale (`extended.md`) — defer verdicts until 2-3 days after refeed.

## Plateau Checklist (run in order, stop at the first hit)

1. **Is it real?** Under 3 weeks flat on the rolling average is not a plateau — it is measurement noise plus a water shift. Wait.
2. **Window creep** — the top silent cause of "fasting stopped working": 16:8 quietly became 14:10. `tracking.md` detects it (≥1h drift, 3+ days); restore the boundary before changing anything else.
3. **Calorie creep inside the window** — fasting caps when, not how much; dense window meals erase the deficit invisibly. One honest week of logging the window's content settles it (`calories` skill).
4. **Weekend pattern** — five weekday deficit days cannot outrun two surplus days; a 2,000 kcal weekend surplus cancels a 300 kcal/day weekday deficit almost exactly. Log weekends as the pattern they are (`tracking.md`).
5. **New maintenance reached** — TDEE falls roughly 15 kcal/day per kg lost (Mifflin-St Jeor weight term × activity multiplier): 10 kg down ≈ 150 kcal/day less burn, so the deficit that started the loss has shrunk under you. Recompute before extending anything.
6. **Only now change one variable** (`protocols.md`): lengthen the fast OR shrink the window OR add one weekly 24h — never together, or the result can't be attributed.

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Answering a stall with longer fasts by default | Escalation loop; hunger debt gets repaid in the window | Run the checklist — step 5 is usually the answer, and rule 7 caps the impulse |
| "Earned it" window meals after a clean fast | The deficit is erased invisibly; the fast becomes a license | Step 3 once; the log ends the argument |
| Daily-weight panic | ±1-2% noise misread as trend, then protocol churn | Rolling average only; verdicts weekly |
| Chasing the extended-fast scale drop | It is glycogen water; it returns by design | Count week-over-week averages across refeeds |
| Cutting protein to shrink window calories | Muscle loss books as scale success, then regains as fat | Hold the `training.md` protein target; cut elsewhere |

## Keeping It Muscle

Protein at the `training.md` target (1.6-2.2 g/kg, ISSN range) plus resistance training 2+ sessions/week. Scale loss without strength work is part muscle — and that regain sets up worse body composition than the starting point. The returning-cycle and training-quality signals (`women.md`, `training.md`) outrank the scale when they conflict with it.
