# Extended Fasts — 24h and Beyond

Operating manual for 24-72h water fasts. Past 72h unsupervised: stop encouraging, advise medical oversight (Red Flags). 5+ days: medical supervision, full stop.

## Before Starting

- Screen `safety.md` FIRST: contraindications and the medication table. A medicated diabetic needs a clinician's fasting plan before hour zero.
- Calendar check: no heavy training, long drives, or high-stakes work inside the fast — day-2 concentration dips are real.
- Last meal: normal size, protein + vegetables + salt. A "last supper" binge makes day 1 hungrier (bigger insulin swing), not easier.
- Optional: a lower-carb week beforehand shortens the day-1-2 adaptation misery. Helpful, never required.
- Buy electrolytes BEFORE starting: nobody shops well at hour 40.

## Electrolyte Protocol (rule 4, operationalized)

Typical daily target during water fasting: sodium 3-5 g, potassium 1-3.5 g, magnesium 300-500 mg.

| Electrolyte | Daily target | Form | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sodium | 3-5 g | Salt in water, sipped through the day | Salt = sodium × 2.5: 3-5 g sodium ≈ 7.5-12.5 g salt (~1.5-2.5 tsp) |
| Potassium | 1-3.5 g | Potassium chloride ("lite salt") or citrate, divided | Large single doses cause GI upset — divide, always |
| Magnesium | 300-500 mg | Glycinate or citrate, split, evening-weighted | Doubles as the insomnia aid (`symptoms.md`) |

- Spread everything across the day: front-loading causes nausea, skipping causes the headache/dizziness/palpitations cluster.
- Unsweetened electrolyte powder keeps the fast under either definition; sweetened versions break strict (`tracking.md`).

## Day-by-Day Expectations

| Window | Typical experience | Play |
|---|---|---|
| 0-24h | Hunger waves at habitual meal times; energy normal | Ride the waves (~20-30 min each); electrolytes running by 24h at the latest |
| 24-48h | The hardest stretch: hunger peak, low energy, irritability, light sleep | Full electrolytes, walks only, early bed. Most aborted fasts die here — say so in advance, it reframes day 2 as the summit |
| 48-72h | Hunger fades (ketone appetite suppression); energy steadies; clarity common | Keep electrolytes going; feeling good is not a reason to add training or extend the target (rule 7) |
| >72h | Outside self-serve scope | Medical oversight (Red Flags) |

## During

- Allowed: water, sparkling water, black coffee, plain tea, salt/electrolytes. Everything else → gray-zone table (`tracking.md`).
- Activity: walking and mobility yes; strength and HIIT no from 24h on (`training.md`).
- Check-ins only with `extended_check_ins: true` — then every 12h: electrolytes taken? anything from `symptoms.md`? still intending the target?
- The scale lies during a fast: 1 g glycogen binds ~3 g water, so early loss is mostly water and returns at refeed (`weight-loss.md`). Say it before they weigh, not after.

## Ending — Planned or Early

- Target reached → `refeeding.md`; portion scaled by rule 5.
- Breaking early is a completed fast of N hours, logged neutrally (rule 7).
- Break NOW, not gently, for: any Red Flags row, stacked symptoms (`symptoms.md`), or inability to keep water down.
