# Women — Cycle, Fertility, PCOS, Menopause

The evidence base for sex-specific fasting rules is thin; logged symptoms outrank any general rule in this file. Pregnancy and breastfeeding are Red Flags rows — no tracking, refer to their provider.

## The Cycle

- Follicular phase (period to ovulation): fasting typically feels easiest — if the user experiments with longer fasts, this is the low-friction slot.
- Luteal phase (ovulation to period): appetite and energy expenditure both rise modestly (on the order of 100-300 kcal/day across studies). Hunger against the same window in this phase is physiology, not failing discipline — say so; it prevents the guilt spiral.
- Some clinicians advise shorter windows in the late-luteal days; trial evidence is thin either way (SKILL.md, Where Experts Disagree). Offer the option when late-luteal logs show repeated struggle; never impose it.

| Signal (from logs) | Read | Play |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle turns irregular after a protocol change | Energy availability dropping | Step down one level (`protocols.md`); re-evaluate in 2 cycles |
| Period absent 3 months, not pregnant, not menopausal | Hypothalamic amenorrhea pattern | Clinician; stop coaching fasts of any length until cleared |
| Sleep and mood crash in the same cycle days repeatedly | Late-luteal pattern | Offer a shortened window for those days; store the preference |
| Repeated hard-fast failures clustered pre-period | Fighting physiology | Move long fasts to the follicular phase |

## Fertility and Pregnancy

- Trying to conceive: aggressive protocols (OMAD, frequent 24h+, large deficits) can suppress the reproductive axis. State it once; if the user continues, keep to mild TRE logging and drop progression talk entirely.
- Confirmed pregnancy or breastfeeding: Red Flags row — stop tracking fasts, no exceptions; hunger there is instruction, not noise.

## PCOS

- Time-restricted eating shows promise for insulin resistance in small PCOS trials — hypothesis-grade evidence: present it as "worth trying with your clinician", never as treatment.
- Commonly co-managed with metformin → medication row in `safety.md` (empty-stomach GI upset is the usual friction).
- Weight loss can restore cycle regularity; track the cycle as an outcome metric alongside weight — a returning cycle is a better success marker than the scale (`weight-loss.md`).

## Perimenopause and Menopause

- Fasting is heavily marketed for menopausal weight regain; the evidence is the same as for everyone — the deficit does the work (`weight-loss.md`). No menopause-specific fasting magic exists; say so kindly.
- Hot flashes and fasting insomnia overlap and get misattributed both ways: before blaming the fast for broken sleep, check whether the pattern predates it (`symptoms.md`).
- Protein at the `training.md` target and resistance training matter more with age, not less — sarcopenia risk rises exactly when aggressive fasting undercuts protein.

## Energy Availability (the stacking risk)

Fasting + heavy training + a calorie deficit stacks low energy availability (the RED-S pattern). The early alarm is an irregular or vanishing cycle — treat it as the signal it is, not an inconvenience to fast through. Two of the three levers can stay; all three at once is the trap.
