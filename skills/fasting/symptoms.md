# Symptoms — What Fasting Discomfort Means

Work symptom-first; each chain is ordered by probability and every step ends in an action. Anything matching the Red Flags table in SKILL.md overrides this file: break the fast, run the Action.

## Headache (usually day 1-2)

1. Sodium loss, not dehydration — the majority of fasting headaches. Fix: ~1-2 g of salt in a glass of water; relief typically inside an hour. Water alone dilutes sodium further and can make it worse.
2. Caffeine withdrawal — if they also dropped coffee. Black coffee is fast-safe; for dry fasts where mid-fast coffee is impossible, taper ahead (`religious.md`).
3. Severe, sudden, or persisting after salt and caffeine are addressed → Red Flags (hyponatremia territory).

## Dizziness / Lightheaded

1. On standing only → orthostatic drop: fluid + salt, rise slowly, sit until it passes. Common past 24h; compounded by BP meds and diuretics (`safety.md`).
2. Not positional, with shakiness/sweating → hypoglycemia pattern: break with 15 g fast carb; a medicated diabetic at <70 mg/dL is the rule 6 Red Flag.
3. Fainting or near-fainting → Red Flags row, no exceptions.

## Hunger

- Waves anchored to old meal times, not a rising line; a wave passes in ~20-30 min. Ride-out tools: water, salt, tea, a walk.
- Hunger that turns constant with weakness past 48h is a body signal, not a test of will: suggest breaking.
- Day 2 of an extended fast is the hardest; hunger typically fades on day 3 as ketones suppress appetite (`extended.md`). Saying this in advance is what gets people through day 2.

## Insomnia / Vivid Dreams

- Elevated adrenaline in deeper fasting; common from 24h. Evening magnesium (within the rule 4 daily target) helps some; mostly, warn ahead — an expected symptom scares nobody.
- Not a red flag alone; paired with palpitations → electrolytes now, Red Flags if it persists.

## Bad Breath / Metallic Taste

- Acetone: benign ketosis marker that tracks BHB rising (`metrics.md`). Water and tongue brushing. Reframe it: the signal they were fasting for.

## Cold Hands and Feet

- Normal thermogenesis downshift on longer fasts. Layers, warm plain tea. Escalate only when paired with confusion or extreme fatigue.

## Muscle Cramps

- Magnesium first, sodium second; split Mg doses for GI tolerance (`extended.md`). Cramps + palpitations together → break and run Red Flags.

## Palpitations / Racing Heart

- Usually skipped electrolytes (K/Mg) — but this symptom gets no troubleshooting ladder: take electrolytes, break the fast, escalate if it persists (Red Flags row).

## Irritability / Brain Fog

- Hours 12-20, before ketones ramp: normal transition window; clears for most as ketosis builds. Fog persisting past 24h despite electrolytes → break. Fasting clarity is the norm; its absence is information.

## Nausea

- Early fast: often coffee on an empty stomach — cut the coffee before cutting the fast.
- Extended fast: electrolyte imbalance or gallbladder complaint; break gently (`refeeding.md`). Recurring right-upper-abdomen pain → clinician (gallstone risk, `safety.md`).
- On an SGLT2 inhibitor, nausea/vomiting/abdominal pain is urgent even with normal glucose (`safety.md`).

## GI Trouble After Breaking

- Bloating, cramps, or diarrhea after the break meal = portion or composition error, not sudden food intolerance: re-apply rule 5 scaling; next break smaller, protein/fat first (`refeeding.md`).

## When Symptoms Stack

Two or more of dizziness, palpitations, confusion, vomiting: stop triaging, break the fast now, clinician if not clearly resolving. The chains above are for single, mild symptoms.
