# Leaving Beijing — The Exit Checklist Nobody Gives You

> Last updated: February 2026 | The reverse of `resident.md`. Money transfer limits: `resident.md` (canonical). Tax records: `taxes.md`.

## The Ordered Checklist (weeks before flight)

### T-8 weeks

- Resign per contract: 30 days written notice is the statutory minimum (`jobs.md`) — but permits, not politeness, set the real timeline below.
- Get shipping quotes (sea freight 6-10 weeks: `resident.md` — it may arrive after you do; decide what travels as luggage).
- School withdrawal notice if you have kids — international schools want a term's notice for deposit return (`education.md`).
- Pets: start export paperwork now — rabies titer test plus destination-specific wait (EU: 3 months after a passing titer). Started late, the pet flies months after you.

### T-4 weeks

- **Work permit cancellation**: the employer files it and receives a cancellation certificate (注销证明). **Get a copy.** Any future China employer needs it, and you cannot obtain it after the company loses interest in you.
- Residence permit reality: once the permit is cancelled, PSB converts your status to a short stay — commonly ~30 days. Book the flight after the cancellation date, not before it.
- **Pension refund**: foreigners leaving for good can withdraw the pension individual-account balance (your 8% employee share — `taxes.md`); apply at the social insurance bureau with passport, departure evidence, and your bank account. Medical individual-account balance is similarly claimable. This is real money after a few years — do not skip it.
- Sell furniture and gear: WeChat neighborhood groups and Xianyu (闲鱼) move things in days; the last week everything goes for nothing.

### T-2 weeks

- **Tax records**: pull IIT payment certificates via the IIT app or tax bureau (`taxes.md`) — the document banks demand for remitting your savings out, and your proof of clean filing forever.
- Transfer money home while employed: the bank process for converting salary savings runs on employment + tax documentation (`resident.md`, the $50k framework) — it is dramatically easier while your contract and salary flow are still verifiable. Move the bulk now, not after the last day.
- Utilities, internet: settle and cancel; landlord walkthrough with your move-in photos (`resident.md`) — the deposit fight is won with evidence, scheduled before your leverage (presence) expires.
- Cancel subscriptions inside apps: Meituan/JD/iQiyi renewals quietly bill a card you can no longer manage from abroad.

### T-1 week and departure

- Bank account: **decide, don't drift.** Closing from abroad later is near-impossible (in-person rules). Either close it now, or deliberately keep it with mobile banking alive — useful if any refund, deposit, or pension payout lands late.
- **Cancel the phone number LAST — or don't cancel it at all.** WeChat, Alipay, bank apps, and every Chinese account authenticate by SMS to that number. Recycled numbers get sold; the buyer can reset "your" accounts. Cheapest keep-alive plan for 6-12 months is the professional move; cancel only after migrating WeChat/bank verification to a home number.
- PSB: no separate exit deregistration exists for you — the work-permit cancellation and permit conversion above are the deregistration. (Your landlord's tax/registration paperwork is their problem, not yours.)

## Documents to Keep Forever

| Document | Why you'll want it |
|---|---|
| Work permit cancellation certificate | Gate for any future China work permit |
| IIT payment records (all years) | Bank remittances, future visa applications, home-country tax defense |
| Social insurance record + refund receipts | Proof of contributions if you ever return or claim under a totalization treaty (`taxes.md`) |
| Reference letter **with company chop** | The chop (公章), not the signature, is what a Chinese verifier trusts |
| Rental contracts + deposit receipts | Deposit disputes surface after you leave |
| Kids' vaccine and school records, bilingual | Next school system will ask; translations cost 10x from abroad |

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Flying out the day after the last workday | Permit cancellation + pension + tax paperwork need you in the country | 2-3 buffer weeks between last workday and flight |
| "I'll close the bank account on my next visit" | Next visit needs a visa; account gets frozen for inactivity meanwhile | Decide at T-1 week: close, or maintain deliberately |
| Cancelling the SIM at the airport | Every account recovery flow dies with the number | Keep-alive plan or full verification migration first |
| Leaving with unfiled reconciliation | Unpaid IIT balances resurface on any future China application | March-June filing done, records downloaded (`taxes.md`) |
| Shipping books/media unexamined | Printed matter is inspected; political content confiscated and flagged | Cull the shelf before the packers come (`safety.md`) |
