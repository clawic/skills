## Having a Baby in Beijing

- Hospital choice and delivery costs are in `healthcare.md` (United Family ¥80,000-150,000; public VIP ¥20,000-50,000). Book the delivery package in the first trimester — international hospitals cap monthly deliveries.
- **Yuesao** (月嫂, live-in newborn specialist for the first month(s)): ¥8,000-20,000/month (`lifestyle.md`); book 3+ months before the due date via agency or hospital referral; check her certificate and ask for references from two prior families.
- Flying while pregnant: most airlines want a doctor's certificate from 28 weeks and refuse boarding around 36 — decide early whether the birth happens in Beijing or at home, because the visa timeline below doesn't compress.
- Check the mother's residence permit expiry against the due date: renewing a permit with a newborn in arms is doable but miserable — renew early (`visas.md`).

## Newborn Paperwork (order matters, each step gates the next)

A baby born in China to two foreign parents is a foreign national with zero documents. The sequence:

1. **Birth medical certificate** (出生医学证明) from the hospital — get 2-3 notarized translated copies while you're at it; every later step wants one.
2. **Passport** for the baby from your embassy (plus home-country birth registration/citizenship paperwork — timelines vary wildly by country; start within days).
3. **Stay or residence permit** from PSB Exit-Entry within **60 days of birth** — statutory window. The baby cannot board an international flight without steps 2-3.
4. Add the baby to insurance and, on the next cycle, to the working parent's dependent visa sponsorship (S1 logic: `visas.md`).

Dual-national wrinkle: if one parent is Chinese, the baby may be treated as a Chinese citizen regardless of the foreign passport — that is a different, harder track (travel document 旅行证 instead of visa); get embassy advice before the birth, not after.

## Hiring an Ayi (household/childcare help)

Rates and roles: `lifestyle.md` (full-time live-out ¥5,000-8,000; hourly ¥40-80). Childcare-specific practice:

- **Channels**: agencies (fee ≈ half to one month's salary, replacements included), WeChat mom groups (references but no safety net), apps like 阿姨来了.
- **Vetting**: ID copy, health certificate (健康证, renewed yearly), 1-week paid trial, references you actually call. For childcare add: watch a full solo afternoon on your home camera before leaving her truly alone — normal and expected here, but tell her the cameras exist (recording a worker covertly poisons the relationship and is legally murky).
- **Norms**: 13th-month pay at Chinese New Year is standard; she goes home for CNY — the whole city's ayi workforce does (`culture.md` calendar), so plan those two weeks with zero help.
- config note: record your ayi arrangement and schedule in `~/Clawic/data/beijing/memory.md` once established — it affects half the family logistics answers.

## Kid Healthcare

- Public community health centers deliver the national vaccine schedule near-free; imported/combination vaccines are private-clinic only, priced per dose — bring your home country's schedule and reconcile with a pediatrician once, in writing.
- Pediatrics: same hospital tiers as `healthcare.md` — international clinics for English-speaking routine care, children's hospitals (北京儿童医院) for serious cases; VIP department for the middle ground.
- Air quality is a kids issue: purifier in the bedroom, N95s sized for children, and an AQI threshold for keeping them indoors — stricter than your own (`climate.md`).

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|------|-------------|------------|
| Missing the 60-day newborn permit window | Overstay penalties attach to a baby's record at first exit | Calendar it from the birth date; embassy passport first, PSB immediately after |
| Assuming public school access | Without Beijing hukou, public school entry for foreign kids is district-dependent and paperwork-heavy | Decide school track before choosing the neighborhood (`education.md`, `neighborhoods-choosing.md`) |
| Hiring ayi on rate alone | Cheapest candidates churn or freelance-double-book | Trial week + references; pay mid-range for childcare roles |
| Both parents on dependent timing | If the S1 holder's sponsor changes jobs, the whole family's permits reset together | Keep 6+ months validity margin on every family member's permit |
