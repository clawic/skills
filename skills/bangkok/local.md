# Bangkok Local Services

Setup-day services and the recurring ones. The pattern that matters: some of these (bank account, home fiber) hinge on your visa/work-permit status, and a "no" is often a branch decision, not a rule — try another.

## SIM & Mobile

Three carriers, similar coverage: **AIS** (widest), **True** (bundled WiFi hotspots), **DTAC**. 4G/5G is fast everywhere central.
- **Tourist SIM** (7-30 days): ฿300-500, 15-30 GB, at the airport arrivals hall or any 7-Eleven.
- **Long-term prepaid**: ฿300-700/month, 20-100+ GB; register with passport. eSIM available on all three.

Providers: 3BB (reliable), True Online (widest), AIS Fibre. Install 1-3 days; some condos are locked to one provider — ask before assuming you can pick.

## Banking (visa-status dependent)

Opening an account as a foreigner is a **branch-by-branch lottery**, not a fixed rule.
- **Bangkok Bank** is the most foreigner-friendly; KBank and SCB have the best apps.
- Bring passport **plus** a work permit OR proof of Thai address (lease/condo book); some branches want an employer letter or a ฿500-10,000 minimum deposit. Refused? Try a mall branch (EmQuartier, Siam Paragon) — they handle foreigners daily.
- **PromptPay** (instant transfer by phone number) and QR pay are universal — Thai banking runs on them; set it up first.
- **ATM fee: ฿220 per foreign-card withdrawal, every bank, every time** → withdraw the max (฿20,000-30,000) per transaction, or carry a fee-reimbursing card (Wise, Charles Schwab). Cards work at malls/restaurants; street food and markets are cash-only.

## Immigration Admin

- **Location**: Government Complex, Chaeng Watthana (north Bangkok). Go 7-8 AM; bring every document plus photocopies; use the QueQ app for a queue ticket.
- **90-day reporting** (all long-stay visa holders): in person (free), online at immigration.go.th (when it works), by mail, or via agent (฿500-1,000). **Late = fine that compounds visa problems** — calendar it. Agents (฿500-2,000) exist for extensions if the queue isn't worth your morning. Visa strategy itself: `visas.md`.

## Mail & Shipping

- **Domestic**: Kerry Express and Flash (cheap, fast, drop-off at any counter). Thailand Post EMS for cheaper international; DHL/FedEx for reliable/expensive.
- Thai addresses confuse foreign senders — most expats ship to the **condo front desk** as the delivery point. Format: name / house-soi / khwaeng, khet / Bangkok + postcode.

## Everyday

- **Laundry**: every soi has a shop, ฿30-50/kg wash-dry-fold, usually next-day; condo coin machines ฿20-40/load.
- **Groceries**: premium imports at Villa Market / Gourmet Market / UFM Fuji (Japanese); mid at Tops / Big C; wholesale at Makro (membership); cheapest produce at the neighborhood fresh market, early morning.
- **Repairs**: phones at MBK 4th floor (dozens of shops, same-day, cheaper than official); computers at Pantip Plaza / Fortune Town; handymen via Seekster app or the building's LINE group.
- **Maid/cleaning**: one-off ฿500-1,000/visit via Seekster; full-time/live-in ฿12,000-18,000/month — vet via building recommendations, not a cold app booking.

## Apps to Install Day One

Grab (transport + food), LINE (the default messenger — Thais expect it, not WhatsApp), Foodpanda (food), IQAir (AQI, `climate.md`), True Money / PromptPay (payments), Shopee/Lazada (delivery), QueQ (immigration queues).
