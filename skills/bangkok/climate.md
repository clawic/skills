# Bangkok Climate & Weather

Hot and humid every month; the variable that reorders a day is not temperature but **rain, heat index, and air quality**. Plan outdoor time around those three, not around the calendar season.

## The Three Seasons (and what each actually costs you)

| Season | Months | Day temp | The real constraint |
|--------|--------|----------|---------------------|
| **Hot** | Mar-May | 34-40°C, heat index >45°C in April | Outdoor only 6-10 AM / after 6 PM; midday walking is a heat-exhaustion risk |
| **Rainy** | Jun-Oct | 32-34°C | Afternoon downpours (2-5 PM), ~30-60 min each; flooding + traffic collapse, not all-day rain |
| **Cool** | Nov-Feb | 25-33°C | The pleasant window — and peak tourist prices, plus the worst PM2.5 |

"Cool" means 25°C mornings, still 32°C by midday. There is no cold season.

## Rain-Day Base Rates (the number that plans a trip)

| Month | Avg high | Rain days | Note |

| Jan | 32°C | 1 | Driest, peak season, worst haze |

| Mar | 34°C | 3 | Heat building |
| Apr | 35°C | 6 | Peak heat + Songkran (`festivals.md`) |
| May | 34°C | 16 | Rains begin |
| Jun-Aug | 33°C | 16-20 | Monsoon core |
| **Sep** | 32°C | **21** | Wettest month; Gulf islands hard to reach |
| Oct | 32°C | 17 | Tapering, still floods |
| Nov | 32°C | 5 | Cooling starts, best all-round month |

Best overall window: **late Nov to early Dec** — rain gone, heat down, haze not yet peaked.

## Air Quality — the underrated planning variable

PM2.5 season is **Dec-Mar, worst Jan-Feb**: regional crop burning + vehicle exhaust trapped in Bangkok's basin. This overlaps the "best weather" months, so a clear cool day can still be AQI 150+.

| AQI (PM2.5) | Action |
|-------------|--------|
| 0-50 | Normal |
| 51-100 | Sensitive groups ease outdoor exercise |
| 101-150 | Everyone cut outdoor exertion; N95 for kids/asthma |
| 151+ | Indoor day; N95 outside; run the air purifier |

Check **before** committing to a temple day or a run, not after — IQAir/AirVisual reads live. Rooms with an air purifier clear in ~30 min; condos on high floors are not immune.

## Heat Rules (act-as)

- Schedule temples and markets **7:30-10 AM** in hot season; the Grand Palace at noon in April is a medical risk, not a photo op.
- Heat index, not the thermometer, is the number — 35°C at 70% humidity behaves like 45°C. Sweat that suddenly stops, dizziness, or nausea = get to A/C and hydrate now; escalate if it doesn't pass (`healthcare.md`).
- Acclimatization takes ~2-3 weeks; the first fortnight is the hardest — pace it, don't power through.

## Rain Rules (act-as)

- Rain = **BTS/MRT, never Grab**: rain triggers surge pricing and gridlock at the same moment (`transport.md`). Rail is unaffected.
- The downpour clears in 30-60 min — duck into a mall or café and wait rather than reroute the day.
- **Known flood chokepoints**: Asok/Sukhumvit 21 underpass, lower Sukhumvit sois, Ratchada. Ankle-to-knee water after a hard storm. Before signing a lease, ask the building's LINE group whether the soi floods (`neighborhoods-choosing.md`).

## Beach-Trip Timing (day/weekend trips out)

- **Gulf coast** (Koh Samui, Phangan): driest Jan-Apr; **avoid Sep-Nov** (their monsoon).
- **Andaman coast** (Phuket, Krabi): best Nov-Apr; rough May-Oct.
- Note these run on *opposite* schedules — same week can be perfect on one coast and washed out on the other.
