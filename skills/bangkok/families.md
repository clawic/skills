# Bangkok with Kids — Schools & Family Life

The ordering rule that governs everything else: **choose school first, home second.** Bangkok school-run traffic doubles commutes; a "15-minute" drive to school is 45+ at 7:30 AM. Families who pick the neighborhood first spend years paying for it in the car.

## International Schools — the Real Cost Tiers

Annual tuition, per child, rising with grade level (secondary = top of each range):

| Tier | Schools | Tuition/year | Notes |
|------|---------|--------------|-------|
| Top tier | ISB, Bangkok Patana, NIST, Shrewsbury, Harrow | ฿700,000-1,000,000+ | Waitlists at ISB/Patana/NIST — apply 6-12 months ahead |
| Mid tier | Ruamrudee, St Andrews, American School of Bangkok, Bromsgrove | ฿300,000-600,000 | Strong academics, shorter waitlists |
| Bilingual / Thai-international | Many (e.g. Sarasas group) | ฿100,000-250,000 | Thai-majority classrooms; genuine bilingualism, lighter facilities |

- One-time costs on top: application ฿3,000-7,000; registration/"development" fee ฿100,000-300,000 at top tier — sunk if you switch schools. Ask if the employer's package covers it before negotiating salary.
- Curriculum choice (British/American/IB) is really a question about where the child will attend university — match the system, not the campus tour.
- Two kids at top tier ≈ ฿2M/year: for many families this single line item decides between Bangkok and home.

## Helpers & Childcare

- Live-in nanny/housekeeper: ฿12,000-18,000/month; live-out ฿15,000-25,000; part-time ฿300-500/day. Word-of-mouth via school parent groups beats agencies on both quality and price; agencies add placement fees but handle replacement.
- Foreign (e.g. Filipina) nannies: common, better English, usually working without permits — the legal risk sits with the employer; know that going in.
- Daycare/preschool from ~18 months: ฿15,000-50,000/month international-standard.

## Healthcare for Kids

- **Samitivej Sukhumvit** runs a dedicated children's hospital — the default pediatric answer; Bumrungrad pediatrics comparable (`healthcare.md` for tiers and insurance).
- Vaccination schedules differ from Western ones (BCG, JE) — reconcile with your home schedule at one pediatrician and keep a single record book.
- Giving birth in Bangkok: private hospital packages roughly ฿50,000-90,000 natural, ฿90,000-180,000 C-section at top hospitals. Birth in Thailand does **not** confer citizenship (jus sanguinis) — register the birth at your embassy for the passport.

## Daily Life with Kids

- Heat rules the schedule the way it rules temple visits: outdoor play 7-10 AM or after 5 PM; the rest is pools and malls — which is why every mall has a play floor (KidZania at Siam Paragon, SEA LIFE, HarborLand branches).
- Parks: Lumphini and Benjakitti for bikes and monitor lizards (a feature, not a bug); Rot Fai Park north for real cycling.
- AQI season (Dec-Mar) hits kids first: check AQI before outdoor plans, N95s that actually fit children, schools publish indoor-day policies — ask for it during admissions (`climate.md`).
- Weekend escapes with kids that work: Hua Hin (calm beach), Khao Yai (space to run), Ancient City (Samut Prakan, rentable golf carts) — `lifestyle.md` for the full list.

## Visas for the Family

- Working parent on Non-B → spouse and kids get Non-O dependent visas riding the work permit (`visas.md`).
- DTV covers spouse and children as dependents — one ฿500k proof per family works; verify current dependent rules.
- School enrollment can anchor ED-based options for the child, with a guardian visa for one parent — the fallback when neither parent has work status.

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|------|--------------|------------|
| Choosing home before school | School-run traffic doubles the map's times | School first; then `neighborhoods-choosing.md` within its catchment |
| Budgeting tuition without the entry fees | ฿100-300k registration per child, non-refundable | Get the full fee schedule in writing before committing |
| Assuming birth = Thai passport | Thailand is jus sanguinis | Embassy birth registration within their deadline |
| Hiring the first agency nanny at week one | No parent-network vetting; premium price | Ask the school's parent LINE group first |
| Ignoring AQI season for a January move-in | Kids arrive straight into the worst air of the year | Time arrival for May-Nov, or land with purifiers pre-ordered |
