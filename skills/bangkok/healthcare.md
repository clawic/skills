# Bangkok Healthcare

Bangkok is a top-tier medical destination: private care matches Western quality at 20-50% of the price, English-speaking, short waits. The real decisions are **which tier to use for what**, and **carry insurance** — a serious admission still runs ฿500,000-2,000,000+ and hospitals want payment secured upfront.

## Where to Go (severity → destination)

| Situation | Go to | Note |
|-----------|-------|------|
| Life-threatening (chest pain, severe bleeding, unconscious) | Call **1669** or go straight to nearest ER | Care given before payment sorted; stabilize first |
| Serious / after-hours | International-hospital 24/7 ER | Bumrungrad, Bangkok Hospital, Samitivej |
| Routine / specialist | Hospital outpatient (walk-in or book) | Specialist waits usually <1 week |
| Minor (cold, rash, refill) | Pharmacy | Pharmacists advise; most drugs OTC |

## Hospital Tiers

| Tier | Hospitals | Trade-off |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **International** | Bumrungrad, Bangkok Hospital, Samitivej (family/expat), BNH, Vejthani | Hotel-like, all English, insurance-friendly; 2-5× Thai-hospital price |
| **Private Thai** | Phyathai, Paolo, Vibharam, Mission | Good care, some English; 30-50% cheaper than international |
| **Public/teaching** | Siriraj, Chulalongkorn, Ramathibodi | Excellent doctors, very cheap, long waits, little English |

**International-hospital cash prices (no insurance)**: consultation ฿1,500-3,000 · ER visit ฿3,000-10,000 · minor surgery ฿50,000-200,000 · major surgery ฿200,000-1,000,000+.

## Insurance (the real recommendation: have it)

| Type | Cost | Fits |
|------|------|------|
| Travel (SafetyWing, World Nomads) | $50-150/month | Short stays, emergency-only |
| Thai local (Pacific Cross, etc.) | ฿15,000-50,000/year | Long-stay, covers Thai hospitals; may exclude pre-existing |
| International (Cigna, Bupa, Aetna) | $1,000-5,000/year | High limits, worldwide, evacuation |

Minimum worth buying: **$100,000 hospitalization + medical evacuation + outpatient**. Without insurance, international hospitals expect a deposit or card before non-emergency treatment.

## Pharmacies

Boots and Watsons (chains, mall) plus independent shops on every soi. **Many prescription drugs sell OTC** — antibiotics, common painkillers, allergy meds — pharmacists speak enough English for minor issues and prices beat the West. Bring documentation and original containers for **controlled substances** (opioids, psychotropics, some sedatives); a 30-day personal supply is generally fine at the border.

## Dental (Bangkok is a bargain here)

| Procedure | Price (THB) |
|-----------|-------------|
| Cleaning | ฿800-1,500 |
| Filling | ฿1,000-2,500 |
| Root canal | ฿8,000-15,000 |
| Crown (porcelain) | ฿10,000-18,000 |
| Implant | ฿40,000-80,000 |

BIDC and Thantakit are the expat-favored centers; hospital dental departments are solid value. Get the quote in writing first.

## Medical Tourism Snapshot

Common draws vs US price: LASIK $1,500-3,000 (US $4,000-6,000) · dental crown $300-500 (US $1,000-2,000) · hip replacement $12,000-20,000 (US $40,000-80,000). Bumrungrad is the flagship. If planning surgery here: confirm the surgeon handles post-op follow-up remotely, and don't book a flight home before the surgeon clears you to fly.

## Annual Checkups & Vaccinations

- **Checkup packages** (much cheaper than home): basic ฿2,500-4,000, comprehensive ฿8,000-40,000 depending on hospital; morning/fasting, results 1-3 days.
- **Vaccinations**: none legally required for most Western travelers; hospital travel clinics and the **Thai Red Cross (Queen Saovabha Institute)** handle Hep A/B, typhoid, tetanus, rabies (if animal-contact risk), Japanese encephalitis (rural travel).

## Mental Health

Psychiatry/psychology at Bumrungrad, Samitivej, Bangkok Hospital (English-speaking, ask when booking); standalone clinics like MindSpa; teletherapy (BetterHelp, Talkspace) works. Psychiatrist ฿2,000-4,000/session, counselor ฿1,500-3,500.
