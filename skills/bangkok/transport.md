# Bangkok Transport

The whole decision compresses to one rule: **if a rail route exists, take it; if not, Grab; short soi hops, motorbike taxi.** Bangkok has among the world's worst traffic, so mode choice — not distance — sets your travel time. A 10 km trip is 15 min by BTS or 90 min by car in rush hour.

## Mode Cheat-Sheet

| Situation | Mode | Cost | Why |
|-----------|------|------|-----|
| Along Sukhumvit/Silom corridor | BTS | ฿17-62 | Fastest, above the gridlock |
| Chinatown, Ratchada, MRT-served | MRT | ฿17-45 | Only rail into the old core |
| Off-rail, door-to-door, luggage | Grab | ฿60-300+ | Fixed price upfront, tracked |
| <2 km into a long soi | Motorbike taxi (orange vest) | ฿20-100 | Beats traffic; agree price first |
| Riverside / old town | Chao Phraya boat | ฿15-50 | Faster than road, scenic |
| To/from Suvarnabhumi | Airport Rail Link | ฿15-45 | Skips airport-road traffic |
| else | Grab | — | Default when no rail and not a soi hop |

## Rail Network (2026)

- **BTS**: Sukhumvit line (Mo Chit↔Kheha), Silom line (National Stadium↔Bang Wa), Gold line (Icon Siam). Interchange at **Siam**.
- **MRT**: Blue line (loop, hits Chinatown/Wat Mangkon + Hua Lamphong), Purple line.
- **Monorails** (opened 2023): Yellow, Pink — extend reach to outer east/north.
- Operating ~6 AM-midnight; trains every 3-8 min. Rush hour (7-9 AM, 5-8 PM) is sardine-packed — let a full train go rather than miss the interchange.

**Card trap**: BTS (Rabbit card) and MRT (separate token/card) still do **not** share a stored-value card. Contactless bank cards now tap in on MRT and some BTS gates — that's the way around carrying two. Key transfers (walk between systems): Asok(BTS)↔Sukhumvit(MRT), Sala Daeng(BTS)↔Silom(MRT), Mo Chit(BTS)↔Chatuchak Park(MRT).

## Grab & Taxis

- **Grab** shows the fare before you book; surges in rush hour and rain. GrabBike (motorcycle) is faster and cheaper than GrabCar for one person. Set the pickup pin precisely — drivers won't call if there's no Thai number.
- **Street taxis**: flag fall ฿35, ~฿6-7/km. The scam is meter refusal near tourist spots ("traffic, 300 flat") — walk away and open Grab or hail a *moving* cab. Airport: use the official taxi queue, tolls paid on top.

## Motorbike Taxis

Orange-vested riders cluster at every soi mouth. Fixed local rates: ~฿20-40 within a soi, ฿40-80 for 1-2 km. **State destination and confirm price before mounting.** Best use: BTS station→home down a 1 km soi in the heat. Skip in heavy rain, with luggage, or for long distances.

## Boats (riverside / old town, no rail there)

Chao Phraya Express — read the **flag**:
- **Orange flag**: the workhorse, express, ฿16 flat, most piers.
- **No flag**: local, every stop, slow.
- **Blue flag**: tourist hop-on/off, ~฿50 or day pass — pricier, English commentary.

Useful piers: Saphan Taksin (BTS link), Tha Tien (Wat Pho), Tha Chang (Grand Palace), Phra Athit (Khao San). Cross-river ferries ฿4-5. Khlong Saen Saep canal boats cut east-west through the centre (฿10-20) — fast, wet, no English; change boats at Pratunam.

## Airport Rail Link

Suvarnabhumi → Makkasan (→MRT) → Phaya Thai (→BTS), ~30 min, ฿15-45, first train 5:30 AM. Beats a ฿300-450 Grab that can take 45-90 min in traffic. (Don Mueang airport has no rail link — bus or Grab.)

## Traffic & Rain Reality

- Rush hours 7-9 AM, 5-8 PM; **Friday evening is the worst of the week**.
- **Any rain collapses the road network and surges Grab simultaneously** — switch to rail the moment it starts, don't wait it out in a cab.
- Known flood underpasses (Asok/Sukhumvit 21) can shut; build in buffer or go rail.
