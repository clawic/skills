# Shopping in Bangkok — Malls, Markets, Tailors

## Where to Buy What

| You want | Go to | Not |
|----------|-------|-----|
| Luxury brands | Siam Paragon, EmQuartier, IconSiam | Anywhere claiming "duty free discount" |
| Everything under one roof | CentralWorld, Terminal 21 (cheap food court included) | — |
| Phone cases, repairs, cheap electronics | MBK (bargain here) | Airport electronics |
| Serious IT / components | Fortune Town (MRT Rama 9), Pantip | MBK for laptops |
| Fashion at wholesale prices | Platinum Mall (Pratunam) — prices drop at 2-3+ pieces | — |
| One-of-everything weekend spectacle | Chatuchak (Sat-Sun; go 9-11 AM before the heat) | Chatuchak at 1 PM in April |
| Homeware, plants, vintage | Chatuchak sections 2-4 and 7; Train Market Ratchada evenings | — |
| Tailoring | Established shops with 2-3 fittings (below) | Any tailor a tuk-tuk brought you to |
| Glasses | Optical chains in any mall: eye test free, quality lenses ฿2,000-6,000, often same-day | Home-country prices |

## Bargaining Rules

- **Where it applies**: markets, MBK stalls, street vendors. Never in malls, chains, or 7-Eleven — asking marks you as a mark.
- **The formula**: counter at 50-60% of the first quote, settle around 70-80%. First quote ฿1,000 → counter ฿500-600 → fair close ฿700-800.
- The walk-away is the only real lever: a genuine "too expensive, thank you" + three steps produces the real price or confirms you had it.
- Smile the whole time — aggressive haggling loses face and the discount together (`culture.md`). Over ฿20-50 differences on small items, just pay; the theater isn't worth it.
- Multi-piece pricing at Platinum and Chatuchak is automatic: ask "how much for three?"

## Tailors

Bangkok tailoring is genuinely good OR a tourist trap, distinguishable by one variable: **time**.

- Real bespoke needs 2-3 fittings across 5-7+ days. "3 suits + 2 shirts, $199, ready tomorrow" = fused canvas, sweatshop fabric, boxy fit — the suit dies at the first dry cleaning.
- Honest range for a decent half-canvas wool suit: ฿15,000-40,000. Below ฿10,000, the fabric alone tells you what happened.
- Choose by portfolio and fabric books (ask for actual S100+ wool mills), not by street touts; the tuk-tuk "special tailor" runs on the same commission circuit as the gem scam (`safety.md`).
- Bring a well-fitting garment to copy — it beats measurements for first-time customers.

## VAT Refund (Tourists)

Worth the paperwork on big purchases only:

1. Spend ≥฿2,000 per store per day at shops displaying "VAT Refund for Tourists"; request the P.P.10 form with your passport at purchase.
2. At the airport BEFORE check-in: customs inspection of goods + forms stamped.
3. After security: refund counter pays out; luxury items ≥฿10,000 each get re-inspected there — carry them on.
4. Math: 7% VAT minus processing fees nets ~4-5%. Below a few thousand baht of refund, the queue costs more than it returns.

## Price Reality Check

- **Cheap in Bangkok**: tailoring, glasses, dental (`healthcare.md`), massage, made-to-measure anything, tropical fruit, local fashion brands.
- **Expensive in Bangkok**: wine and imported spirits (import taxes roughly triple the price — a $12 bottle runs ฿1,200+), cheese and imported groceries, books in English, electronics are NOT cheap (≈ home price + 7% VAT; grey-market at MBK is cheaper but warranty-free). Bring laptops and cameras from home.
- Counterfeits are openly sold (Patpong night market exists for this); buying is your home country's customs problem at re-entry — seizure and fines are real for multiples.

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|------|--------------|------------|
| "Gem sale, government promotion, today only" | The most documented scam in Thailand — resale value near zero | Never buy gems to resell; `safety.md` |
| 24-hour suit deals | Fused construction, poly-blend "wool" | Time + fittings + fabric books, ฿15k floor |
| Buying electronics without checking Thai warranty | Grey imports void local service | Ask "Thai warranty?" — price difference is the answer |
| Haggling in fixed-price shops | Marks you as fresh; wastes goodwill | Markets only |
| Checking in VAT-refund luxury goods | Airside re-inspection required ≥฿10,000/item | Carry them through security |
