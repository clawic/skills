# Buying Property in Bangkok

The prime directive: **rent for 12 months in the exact area (ideally the exact building) before buying anything.** Bangkok punishes bought-sight-unseen harder than most cities — oversupply, illiquid resale, and micro-location effects (one soi = quiet, next soi = nightclub bass until 2 AM).

## What a Foreigner Can Own

| Structure | Reality |
|-----------|---------|
| Condo, freehold | YES — within the 49% foreign quota of each building's floor area; full title, sellable, inheritable |
| Land / house with land | NO — foreigners cannot own land (BOI ฿40M+ investment exception is not for normal buyers) |
| 30-year lease | Legal, registrable; "renewable 30+30" clauses are promises, not enforceable rights — value it as 30 years |
| Thai company holding land for you | Nominee structures are illegal (Land Code s.96 bis) and periodically cracked down on; lawyers selling this are selling you the liability |
| Thai spouse owns | Legal; the foreigner signs away claim to the land at transfer — it is genuinely the spouse's |

## The Foreign-Quota Mechanics

- Purchase funds must arrive **from abroad, in foreign currency**, into a Thai account; the bank issues a Foreign Exchange Transaction (FET) form / credit advice for equivalents ≥ $50,000. No FET = the Land Department will not register a foreign-quota condo. Plan the transfer chain before wiring.
- Tax check before the wire: a large remittance of post-2024 income can itself be taxable (`taxes.md`) — sequence grandfathered funds first.
- Verify remaining foreign quota with the juristic office before deposit; popular buildings run out.

## Prices and the Rent-vs-Buy Math

- Central Bangkok condos: roughly ฿100,000-250,000/m²; a new 35 m² 1BR on Sukhumvit ฿4-8M.
- Gross rental yields 4-6%, net 3-4% after fees, vacancy, agent months.
- Break-even formula: **price ÷ annual rent for the same unit = years of rent bought upfront.** ฿5M unit renting at ฿20,000/month → 5,000,000 ÷ 240,000 ≈ 21 years, before fees and with zero appreciation assumed — and central-Bangkok resale prices have been flat-to-negative across much of the last decade due to chronic oversupply.
- Conclusion the numbers force: buy for use (you'll live in it 5+ years and want to), not as an investment thesis.

## Transaction and Holding Costs

| Cost | Amount | Who pays |
|------|--------|----------|
| Transfer fee | 2% of registered value | Split by negotiation, commonly 50/50 |
| Specific business tax | 3.3% if seller owned <5 years | Seller (in law; watch contracts) |
| Stamp duty | 0.5% (only when SBT doesn't apply) | Seller |
| Withholding tax | Sliding scale on seller | Seller |
| Common area fees | ฿40-80/m²/month, prepaid yearly | Owner |
| Sinking fund | ฿500-800/m² one-time at new-build handover | Buyer |
| Land & building tax | Minor for owner-occupied (fractions of a percent) | Owner |

## Off-Plan and New-Build Risks

- No mandatory escrow: your installments finance the developer. Completion risk is real for small developers — stick to listed names (Sansiri, AP Thai, Ananda, Land & Houses, Origin) if buying off-plan at all.
- The rendered pool deck and the delivered one diverge; contracts specify materials vaguely. Resale units in a 5-year-old building show you the truth for the same money.
- "Guaranteed rental return 6% for 2 years" = the guarantee is priced into what you paid; year 3 meets the real 4% market.

## Process (Resale Condo)

1. Agent-optional: sellers pay commission (3%), so buyers can use one freely; or go direct via Hipflat/DDproperty listings.
2. Due diligence (lawyer, ฿20,000-50,000): title deed (chanote) check, foreign quota confirmation, juristic person debt check — unpaid building fees transfer with the unit.
3. Deposit 5-10% with a contract naming the FET condition.
4. Wire in foreign currency → FET forms → transfer day at the Land Department: fees paid, name on chanote same day.

## Traps

| Trap | Why it fails | Do instead |
|------|--------------|------------|
| Buying where you holidayed | Vacation area ≠ living area; you saw December, not April or flood season | Rent there 12 months first, through both seasons |
| Skipping the juristic debt check | Building arrears attach to the unit at transfer | Lawyer requests a debt-free letter before transfer day |
| Wiring baht from an offshore baht account | No FET → no foreign-quota registration | Send foreign currency; convert inside Thailand |
| Valuing 30+30+30 leases as 90 years | Renewals bind nobody after year 30 | Price it as a 30-year decaying asset |
| "My girlfriend's company will hold the house" | Nominee = illegal + the asset is legally not yours | Condo in your name, or lease, or accept renting |
