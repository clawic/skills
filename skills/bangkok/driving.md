# Driving & Vehicles in Bangkok

Default answer: don't. Rail + Grab beats ownership for most residents (car ownership runs ฿17,000-36,000/month all-in — `cost.md`), and Thailand's roads are among the world's deadliest: ~25-30 deaths per 100,000/year (WHO), 70-80% of them motorcyclists. Drive/ride only with a reason (family logistics, weekend trips, living off-grid from BTS).

## Legal to Drive — License Ladder

| Situation | What's valid |
|-----------|--------------|
| Tourist, weeks | Home license + IDP (1949 convention) — home license alone is NOT valid |
| Staying >3 months | Convert to a Thai license — insurers dispute IDP claims on long stays |
| Riding any motorbike | Motorbike is a **separate license class**; a car license (Thai or foreign) does not cover it |

- No valid license for the class = insurance void on top of the fine. This is the detail that turns a scraped knee into a self-funded hospital bill.
- Helmet law applies to driver AND passenger; tourist-area checkpoints fine ฿400-1,000 on the spot for no-helmet or no-license.

## Getting a Thai License

At a Department of Land Transport (DLT) office — main Bangkok office at Chatuchak; book via the DLT Smart Queue app.

1. **Residence certificate**: from Immigration (Chaengwattana, free-฿500, allow 1-2 weeks) or your embassy (same-day, typically $50-80 equivalent).
2. **Medical certificate**: any clinic, ฿100-200, 10 minutes.
3. **Physical tests at DLT**: color blindness, reaction time, peripheral vision, depth perception.
4. With a valid foreign license + IDP, the written and practical tests are **waived** — the whole visit is a half day. Without one, expect the 5-hour training video and both tests.
5. First license = 2-year temporary; renew into a 5-year. Car and motorbike are two separate applications — do both in one visit if you'll ever ride.

## Renting

- **Motorbike/scooter**: ฿200-400/day, ฿2,500-4,500/month for a Click/PCX class. Photograph every scratch on video before riding off; never leave your passport as deposit (cash ฿2,000-5,000 is normal — the passport-hostage damage scam is in `safety.md`).
- **Car**: airport majors (Avis, Budget, local Thai Rent A Car) ฿900-1,800/day; insist on class 1 insurance in the contract.
- City reality: a rented scooter in Bangkok traffic is how visitors end up in `healthcare.md`. If you must ride, learn in a quiet province first, not on Sukhumvit.

## Buying

- **Motorbike**: new Honda Click 125 / PCX 160 ฿55,000-95,000. The **green book** is the title — no green book, no legal ownership, no sale. Transfer at DLT with buyer + seller (or signed power of attorney). Financing generally wants a work permit.
- **Car**: Thai-assembled models (Toyota, Honda, Isuzu) price near global norms and hold resale value; imports carry 200-300% duty — a mid-trim European sedan costs double its home price. Used market is liquid and priced accordingly (blue book: one2car, Kaidee listings as reference).
- Annual keep-alive: road tax + compulsory insurance (Por Ror Bor, ฿600-1,600) + voluntary insurance — class 1 (all-risk) ฿15,000-25,000/year for a normal car, and worth it: class 3-only against Bangkok traffic is self-insuring the expensive half.

## On the Road

- Left-hand traffic. Lane discipline is advisory; motorbikes filter everywhere including footpaths; the biggest vehicle has right of way in practice.
- Police checkpoints are routine, especially evenings and near nightlife sois. Minor offense: ticket ฿500-1,000, license held, pay at the named station to retrieve it. Stay polite and bored — argument escalates, patience discounts (`culture.md` on face).
- Fuel: gasohol 95 around ฿35-45/liter (drifts with oil prices). Bangkok parking: malls free 2-4 hours with stamp; condos often charge for a second vehicle slot.
- Songkran week (mid-April) and New Year are the "seven dangerous days" — national road-death peaks. Don't plan long drives then (`festivals.md`).

## When a Car Actually Wins

- Two+ kids on a school run a soi away from BTS (`families.md`)
- Weekend pattern: Khao Yai / Hua Hin / Kanchanaburi trips 3+ times a month — rental math flips around there (3 weekend rentals ≈ ฿10,000-15,000 vs ownership's fixed ฿17,000+)
- Living beyond the rail grid by choice (Bang Na outskirts, Nichada Thani)
