# Links & Authority

## Internal Linking

- Every important page within 3 clicks of the homepage — click depth correlates with crawl frequency and perceived importance
- Descriptive anchors ("SEO audit checklist", not "click here") — internal anchors are the one place exact-match is safe: you control them and Google expects them
- Contextual in-content links pass more value than nav/footer boilerplate
- New page → immediately link to it from your 2-3 strongest related pages; orphans get crawled late and rank late
- Fix broken internal links before any outreach — reclaimed authority with zero outreach cost

## Redirects

- 301s no longer lose PageRank (Google confirmed, 2016) — the old "10-15% loss" rule is dead; consolidating pages via 301 is safe
- Keep chains ≤3 hops; long chains slow crawling and may be abandoned
- Redirect to the closest matching page, never mass-redirect to the homepage — Google treats irrelevant redirects as soft 404s and passes nothing

## External Anchor Text (links pointing at you)

- Exact match: powerful and the first pattern link-spam systems check — keep it a small minority
- Partial match and branded: the bulk of a natural profile
- Naked URLs and generic ("read more"): natural filler, expected in real profiles
- A profile dominated by exact-match commercial anchors reads as manipulation regardless of link quality

## Nofollow, Sponsored, UGC

- Since 2019, nofollow is a hint, not a directive — Google may still count it
- `rel="sponsored"` for paid placements, `rel="ugc"` for comments and forums
- The violation is undisclosed paid follow links; correctly-attributed paid links are policy-compliant and still carry referral and brand value

## Evaluating a Link Prospect

Better test than domain rating alone: does the linking PAGE itself rank and receive traffic? A link from a page with search traffic beats a link from a high-DR site's orphaned post. Topical relevance of the site multiplies value — an SEO-blog link helps an SEO site more than a higher-authority cooking blog would.

## Link Building (White Hat)

- Linkable assets: original data, free tools, calculators, research — things journalists and writers cite without being asked
- Digital PR: newsworthy angles built from your own data
- Journalist requests: HARO shut down in 2024 — use successors such as Source of Sources, Qwoted, or Featured
- Broken link building: find dead resources in your niche, offer your page as the replacement
- Resource page outreach; guest posts on relevant sites with a contextual in-body link (author-bio-only links are worth little)

## Penalties & Disavow

- Buying links, PBNs, scaled exchanges → manual actions; recovery takes months after cleanup plus a reconsideration request
- Disavow only with a manual action naming links, or an unmistakable spam attack — Google ignores most spam links automatically, and a careless disavow cuts real equity
- Disavow at domain level for spam sites (`domain:spamsite.com`); effects take weeks to months
