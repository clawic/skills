# Certifications — Long Horizons Alongside a Job

Working adults rarely fail cert exams; they fail to ever sit them. The design problem is adherence over months of fragmented time, not raw study technique.

## The Frame

- Book the exam date FIRST (reschedulable where the vendor allows). The date creates the countdown everything hangs on; "I'll book when I'm ready" is the perpetual-prep trap.
- Horizon of 2-6 months: Rule 2's ratio drifts down toward 5-10% — weekly relearning slots per topic, not daily cramming.
- The vendor blueprint (published domain weightings) IS the syllabus. Allocate hours by blueprint percentage × current weakness — the `scheduling.md` formula with the vendor's weights plugged in.
- `education_level: professional-cert` in config.yaml routes planning here by default.

## Weekly Shape (job-compatible)

- Default: `study_days_per_week` slots of `block_length` at the same time daily — same-time beats more-time for adherence over months.
- New material goes to the freshest slots (mornings, weekends); post-work evenings default to review-only — unless memory.md shows this student runs otherwise.
- Fragments (commute, lunch) follow the `focus.md` fragment table: reviews only.
- One weekend slot is reserved for full-length practice sets; weekday slots are too short to simulate the exam.

## Question Banks

- Banks with explanations are the core tool. Every miss: read the explanation, then write the card or question YOURSELF — generation encodes, copying doesn't.
- Only exam-condition runs count as signal: timed, closed-book, full-length. Untimed open-book bank runs overestimate readiness (format-mismatched practice inflates confidence — SKILL.md, T-1 week).
- Coverage trap: finishing the bank is not the criterion; the bank samples the blueprint. Track per-domain scores against blueprint weights and re-allocate with the formula.
- Braindumps (leaked real questions): an ethics violation that can void the credential, and they train recognition of specific wordings, not the skill. Refuse them; use legitimate banks.

## Readiness and Booking

- Keep the booked date when two consecutive full-length timed sets clear the published pass mark — both sets, not their average; one good set is variance.
- Below the mark at T-2 weeks with a reschedulable exam: reschedule once and re-plan from the domain scores. A second reschedule needs a concrete changed circumstance, or it is the perpetual-prep trap returning with paperwork.

## Proctoring Logistics

- Online-proctored: room scan, ID rules, and whiteboard policies differ by vendor — read the vendor's current rules the week before, not the morning of.
- Test center vs online: pick whichever removes this student's larger anxiety source; record the choice and how it went in memory.md → Exams.

## After the Cert

- Renewal and CE cycles go on the calendar the day the result arrives — the renewal crammed in a panic three years later is this same file, skipped.
- Post-mortem to memory.md: blueprint accuracy, bank quality, hours actually needed vs planned. The next cert's estimate comes from this log, not from forum anecdotes.
