# Memory Template — Career

Files live in `~/Clawic/data/career/`. Two files: `profile.md` (user-declared facts) and `memory.md` (agent-observed patterns). Never merge them; an observation never overwrites a declaration.

## profile.md

```markdown
# Career Profile

## Status
status: ongoing
last: YYYY-MM-DD

## Comp History
<!-- YYYY-MM · company · level · base / bonus / equity per year · notes -->

## Market Band
<!-- band with sources and date; refresh at every market check -->

## Constraints
<!-- location, remote needs, visa status, family, excluded industries -->

## Values Ranking
<!-- what "better" means, in order: comp, learning, autonomy, mission, hours -->

## Active Targets
<!-- target comp, target level or role, timeline -->

## Decisions Log
<!-- YYYY-MM · decision · rationale · outcome when known -->

---
*Updated: YYYY-MM-DD*
```
