# Layoffs, Firings, And PIPs

Legal specifics below are US defaults; `region` in config switches statutory-notice and severance assumptions. Lawyer threshold: equity at stake, visa at stake, or suspected discrimination → flat-fee employment-lawyer review before signing anything; the review costs less than any one of those mistakes.

## Layoff: The First 48 Hours

1. Sign nothing on day one. US workers 40+ get 21 days to consider a severance release (45 for group layoffs) under OWBPA; taking days to review is normal, not adversarial. Outside the US, statutory notice periods and severance formulas apply — check them before signing.
2. Before access is cut: lock 2-3 reference commitments, export contacts, your comp history, and your own artifact list — never employer IP.
3. File for unemployment immediately; severance rarely blocks eligibility and delays cost real money. If termination is being recorded "for cause", see Fired below first.
4. Health coverage (US): COBRA continues employer coverage up to 18 months at your cost — negotiate employer-subsidized months (below) before comparing marketplace plans.
5. Only then start the search (job-search). A same-day panic application spree with a stale resume converts worse than a 3-day setup with a targeted list.

## Severance Negotiation

- Negotiable exactly once, before signing. Standard asks: extra weeks; healthcare/COBRA subsidy; equity acceleration or an extended option-exercise window (`equity.md`); narrowing or removal of non-compete and non-solicit language; agreed reference language; a LATER termination date — it extends vesting, benefits, and visa clocks at near-zero cost to them.
- Group layoffs flex less on weeks (parity risk across the group) and more on dates, references, and equity mechanics — ask where they CAN move instead of pushing where they can't.
- Mass layoffs (US): the WARN Act requires 60 days notice from employers of 100+ for qualifying mass layoffs and closures; pay in lieu is common — verify the notice period was actually covered.
- Read the release for: non-disparagement scope, clawback triggers, cooperation clauses with unbounded time, and any admission language. Every one of these is edit-able before signing and fixed after.

## Visa Clocks

- US H-1B: 60-day grace period from the last day of employment to transfer, change status, or depart. The layoff timeline IS the visa timeline — negotiate the termination DATE, not just severance weeks.
- `visa_dependent: true` in config: apply immigration clocks to every recommendation in this file, including whether spending days negotiating is affordable at all.

## PIP

- Read a PIP as an exit signal by default: it is documentation. Survivors exist; plan both forks from day one and start the external search immediately regardless — a market check takes longer than a PIP window.
- Fork A (fight): choose it only when the named gap is concrete, achievable inside the window, and your manager wants you to survive. Test: will they meet weekly and confirm progress in writing? A manager who won't commit to that has already chosen.
- Fork B (negotiate exit): PIPs convert to severance more often than people expect — "I'd rather we part cleanly than run this process" opens it; the company saves the management cost and the risk.
- On either fork: sign acknowledgments of RECEIPT, never of the characterization. "Acknowledging receipt, not agreement" next to the signature is standard and accepted.

## Fired (Not Laid Off)

- For-cause vs performance termination decides unemployment eligibility and the reference story. Ask what is being recorded and whether they will contest unemployment.
- Negotiate the record: companies frequently agree to neutral-reference language or a recorded resignation in exchange for a clean release — it costs them nothing.
- Line up references who predate the ending, and where possible agree the story with your manager before the last day.

## The Narrative Afterward

- Layoff, one sentence, factual and forward-facing: "The company cut X% and my org was affected; I'm targeting Y." Layoffs read as market events, not verdicts — deliver it without apology and pivot to the target role.
- Firing or PIP exit: never lie (back-channels and background checks exist), never volunteer the PIP. "The role and my strengths diverged; here's what I'm looking for" is honest and sufficient.
- Gap longer than ~6 months: fill it with named activity — contract work, a shipped project, a credential (`pivots.md`, Career Breaks And Re-entry).
