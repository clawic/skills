# Burnout — Where Optimization Makes It Worse

The constraint: efficiency advice into a depleted system accelerates the collapse. Burnout is described in ICD-11 as an occupational phenomenon with three dimensions — energy depletion, mental distance or cynicism about the job, and reduced professional efficacy — and all three are properties of the relationship between the person and the work, not of the person's technique.

**Before doing anything**, read `## Friction`, `## Energy Patterns` and `## Constraints` in `~/Clawic/data/productivity/memory.md`, and check SKILL.md Red Flags. If depression signals are present — loss of interest across everything, not just work — this file is the wrong one and a clinician is the answer.

## Recognizing It

| Signal | Distinguishing feature |
|---|---|
| Exhaustion sleep does not fix | Tiredness recovers over a weekend; depletion does not |
| Cynicism about work that used to matter | Distance and irritation aimed specifically at the job |
| Feeling ineffective despite the same output | Efficacy judgment drops before the output does |
| Sunday dread starting Saturday morning | Anticipation, not the work itself, is doing the damage |
| Physical symptoms: headaches, insomnia, frequent illness | The body reports before the person accepts it |
| Interest intact outside work | Points at burnout rather than depression — a useful, imperfect distinction |
| Nothing produces interest, including things that used to | Beyond this file: clinician (Red Flags) |

Two or three dimensions present for weeks means stop optimizing. Adding a system at this point produces one more thing to fail at, and that failure is expensive.

## What Fails Here

- **More efficiency.** A better system pours faster into a cracked container. It also confirms the belief that the person is the defective part.
- **Push through.** The mindset that produced the state cannot resolve it; effort is the input that is already exhausted.
- **Weekend recovery.** Two days cannot undo five. If weekends are load-bearing for survival, the weeks are the problem and the weekend is a painkiller.
- **The vacation cure.** Two weeks off returns you to the identical conditions, usually with a backlog. The relief lasts days.
- **Toxic positivity.** "You've got this" dismisses a real, structural finding and closes the conversation exactly where it was becoming useful.
- **Guilt-managed rest.** Resting while believing you should be working produces neither rest nor work.

## What Works

- **Subtraction, first and largest.** What can stop entirely — a project, a committee, a client, a volunteer role, a standing meeting? Empty space is the intervention; everything else is a support for it (`overload.md`).
- **Minimum viable everything.** Every deliverable to C-tier for a defined period, deliberately and without apology. The work will mostly not be noticed, which is itself informative.
- **Physical first.** Sleep, food, daylight, movement. Cognitive symptoms clear only after the body recovers, and no amount of scheduling substitutes (`energy.md`).
- **Hard boundaries with a stated end.** No email after 18:00, no weekend work, for the next eight weeks. Time-bounded boundaries are easier to state and easier for others to accept.
- **Non-productive pleasure.** Something with no output attached, reintroduced deliberately. Anhedonia recedes slowly and only with exposure.
- **A named recovery period.** Weeks to months, not days. Saying the timeframe out loud prevents the second collapse that follows the premature return.
- **One conversation with whoever controls the load.** Manager, client, partner, co-founder. Recovery without a change in the load is a pause, not a recovery.

## Structural or Personal

Burnout is usually a systemic problem wearing individual clothes. Sorting matters because the interventions are entirely different.

| Ask | Structural | Personal |
|---|---|---|
| Are colleagues in the same state? | Yes → structural | No → look closer at load and boundaries |
| Was the load survivable a year ago? | No, it grew → structural | Yes, unchanged → capacity or life load changed |
| Does saying no have a real cost? | Yes → structural | No → boundary skill is the gap (`delegation.md`) |
| Is the work meaningful to you? | Meaning lost → often values mismatch | Meaning intact → load problem, fixable |
| Would a competent replacement burn out too? | Yes → the role is designed wrong | No → something specific to this situation |

Where it is structural, say so plainly and once: some of this is not fixable from inside a calendar. Then work on what is available — the load conversation, the reduced tier, the boundary — without campaigning for a resignation the user has not raised.

## The Return

Coming back too fast is how the second episode happens, and the second is usually worse.

- **Return at reduced capacity on purpose.** Set `focus_hours_target` to 1-2 and commit to it in writing for a defined number of weeks.
- **Add one thing at a time**, with a week between additions. Adding back everything in the first fortnight reproduces the original conditions.
- **Keep one boundary permanently.** The one that mattered most. Everything else can flex; that one becomes the marker of the change.
- **Watch the earliest signal.** For most people it is cynicism, not exhaustion — it appears first and is easier to notice in speech than in energy.
- **Schedule the check-in.** A `## Due` row at six weeks, asking the three ICD-11 dimensions again. Without it, drift back is invisible until it is complete.

## The Real Issue

Burnout is a mismatch between demands and resources, sustained over time, and its most common cause is a job that requires more than one person can supply. Productivity technique cannot fix an under-resourced role, an absent manager, or a culture where boundaries have a cost. Sometimes the honest answer is that the situation has to change, and saying that once — plainly, without pressure — is more useful than any system this skill could build.

## What to Write Down

- The current reduced capacity, with the date it was set and the date to revisit, goes to `config.yaml` (`focus_hours_target`, and `never_negotiable` under `safety_posture`).
- What was cut, who was told, and when goes into the current `reviews/<year>.md` entry — this list is what prevents everything quietly returning.
- The structural findings — which demands exceed the role, which conversations are pending — go to `## Friction`, phrased as observations.
- The recovery check-in goes to `## Due` at six weeks.
- A diagnosis, medication or clinician-set restriction the user states goes to the shared `~/Clawic/data/health/profile.md` as one dated line. Never write an inferred symptom, and never record a clinical impression of your own.
