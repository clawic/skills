# Founder — Everything Is Yours, So Nothing Gets Depth

The founder constraint: no external prioritization at all. Every function — product, sales, support, hiring, finance — has a legitimate claim on the same hours, and the only filter is one person's judgment applied while tired. Runway makes the mistake expensive: unlike a job, the wrong month cannot be worked off later.

**Before advising**, read `## Goals`, `## Commitments` and `## Constraints` in `~/Clawic/data/productivity/memory.md`, and the shared `~/Clawic/data/projects/`. A founder's priority question is answerable only against what was already committed this quarter.

## What Fails Here

- **Everything is urgent.** With no external ranking, recency and emotional salience take over: the loudest customer and the newest idea win by default.
- **Founder martyrdom.** "Nobody else can do this" builds a company that cannot operate without you, which is also the company nobody will buy or fund.
- **Shiny object switching.** Each new market, feature or partnership resets the clock on the previous one; three half-built bets beat zero shipped bets in feeling and lose to them in every other way.
- **Building before selling.** Building is comfortable and measurable; selling risks a no. Months of product work against zero validated demand is the most common expensive mistake.
- **Working in the business.** Support tickets and operations fill every hour available, and they feel productive because they are real. They are also the work most amenable to being someone else's.
- **Hiring to escape the queue.** Hiring under overload usually produces the wrong role, hired too fast, managed by someone with no time to manage.

## What Works

- **One thing per week.** The single outcome that moves the company. Everything else is maintenance. Written down on Monday, checked on Friday (`planning.md`).
- **A CEO day.** One day weekly with no customer or delivery work: metrics, strategy, hiring, writing. It is the first thing to be cancelled and the first thing to defend.
- **Default delegation.** The first question about any task is "who else could do this?", not "how do I do this?" (`delegation.md`).
- **Talk to customers before building.** Sell first, build second. A signed intent is data; a positive conversation is not.
- **Timeboxed experiments.** "Two weeks, this budget, this success criterion, then we decide." A bet with an end date cannot become a permanent drain.
- **Write the operating manual early.** How decisions get made, what gets escalated, what "done" means. It is what makes the first hires productive instead of dependent (`artifacts/operating-manual.md`).

## Runway Arithmetic

`months of runway = cash ÷ net monthly burn`. Everything below follows from that number, and the founder who cannot say it from memory is planning without the only constraint that is genuinely fixed.

- Under 6 months: the only allowable work is revenue, fundraising, or cutting burn. Anything else is a hobby with a good story.
- 6-12 months: one bet at a time, with a decision date inside the runway, not after it.
- Over 12 months: the runway is not the constraint; focus is.
- **An hour spent on low-impact work is runway burned**, and unlike in a funded job, it does not come back. This is the honest version of "time is the one resource you cannot raise more of" — you can raise money, but not the months already spent.

## Prioritizing Without a Boss

Weekly, in this order:

1. **Is there an existential threat?** Cash, a key customer leaving, a legal or security event. It outranks everything, and only one thing at a time can hold this rank.
2. **What is the current constraint?** Usually one of: not enough leads, poor conversion, product does not retain, delivery cannot keep up. Work on the constraint; work anywhere else produces nothing systemic.
3. **What compounds?** Content, referral loops, a hire, a fixed process — these pay in later months and are always the first to be dropped under pressure.
4. **What is loud?** Answered last, and often it can be answered by a template or a decision criterion instead of by you.

The trap this ordering prevents: doing the work of the function you are best at, rather than the function that is limiting the company. Founders reliably over-invest in their own strength.

## Context Switching Is the Job

- Switching is unavoidable here, so batch by mode rather than trying to eliminate it: sales calls together, product work together, admin together. Two modes a day is achievable; six is a wasted day (`focus.md`).
- Protect one deep block in the peak window and accept that the rest of the day is reactive. Trying to make the whole day deep produces zero deep hours and a sense of failure.
- Write decisions down as they are made, in the project file. A founder answers the same question three times a quarter, and the written answer is what stops the fourth (`artifacts/`).

## The Real Issue

Founder productivity problems are prioritization problems: with no external structure, every option competes equally, and the skill is saying no to genuinely good things. The second-order issue is identity — when the company is the person, cutting scope feels like a personal reduction, which is why founders overcommit and then work through the consequences. Both are addressed by the same move: one written bet per period, with a date and an explicit list of what is not being done.

## What to Write Down

- Each bet becomes a project file in the shared `~/Clawic/data/projects/` — hypothesis, success criterion, decision date, outcome. The rejected options and why belong in the same file; that is what stops the idea returning next quarter.
- Quarterly outcomes go to `## Goals`; the weekly one-thing goes into the current `reviews/<year>.md` entry.
- Investors, key customers and advisors go to `~/Clawic/data/contacts/contacts.md` with name, role, channel and one line of context; commitments to them to `## Commitments`.
- The operating manual, decision criteria, and hiring scorecards go to `~/Clawic/data/productivity/artifacts/` with their `## Boxes` lines.
- CEO day, weekly metric review and the runway check become `## Due` rows. Financial detail beyond the runway number is out of this skill's scope and is not written by it.
