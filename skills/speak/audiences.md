# Audiences And Contexts — Who Is Listening, Where

The same content ships differently per listener and situation. Stated context preferences persist under the `contexts` area; infer in-session context from what the user says ("I'm driving"), never by interrogation.

## Hands-Free / Driving

- Attention is borrowed from the road: 2-option ceiling — below even the 3 of `dialogue.md`, exception marked there for the same reason (choice load).
- No spelled-out codes, addresses, or URLs — "sent to your phone" replaces them; never require reading a screen to complete a spoken task.
- Confirmation over information: "Done." beats a summary.

## Non-Native Listeners

- One rate step slower (the same ~10% step as SKILL.md Quick Reference); cut idioms and phrasal verbs before simplifying grammar ("figure out" → "understand") — over-simplified grammar reads as condescension.
- Numbers hit harder: pause before and after figures; single digits over compound number words for anything confirmable.

## Screen-Reader Users

- They own their TTS. Deliver clean, well-punctuated TEXT and let their reader speak it: no phonetic respellings, no pre-normalized "5 dollars 99" strings, no stripped structure — headings and lists are their navigation.
- This inverts most of this skill: the speech rules apply when YOU synthesize, not when their reader does (SKILL.md, Where Experts Disagree — verbatim school).

## Children and Older Listeners

- Children: sentences well under the 20-word cap — aim around 12 words, concrete vocabulary, one instruction per sentence.
- Age-related hearing loss takes high frequencies first: prefer lower-pitched voices for intelligibility over "friendly" high-pitched ones; base rate, never faster.

## Shared and Public Spaces

- Canonical privacy home: nothing sensitive aloud — codes, balances, medical content, third-party message bodies. Headline plus "details in text" (`notifications.md` applies this to alerts; SKILL.md gate 7 enforces it per utterance).
- Uncertain who can hear → act as if public. The cost asymmetry is total: a withheld detail is a follow-up question; a spoken secret is unrecallable.

## Noisy Environments

- Front-load (SKILL.md rule 2) and repeat the one load-bearing figure once at the end; assume the middle was partially lost.
- After one "what?", offer text unprompted — a third spoken repetition is worse than switching channel (`dialogue.md` recovery ladder, same threshold logic).
